Core vaccine for shelter dogs

Considerations

  • Intranasal modified live BAP or BP vaccination is strongly recommended at or before intake for all shelter dogs over 3 weeks of age, and is recommended as a situational vaccine for at-risk community dogs, such as those being temporarily kenneled, attending daycare, or interacting with symptomatic dogs, and those who must be vaccinated to receive essential services, such as grooming. The parainfluenza component is especially important for preventing outbreaks in settings where dogs are intensively housed.
  • Intranasal tri- or bivalent products are preferred over the multivalent oral product for shelter dogs at this time, due to lack of field trials for the oral product.
  • The formulation and route of Bordetella+/-Parainfluenza+/-Adenovirus vaccines strongly inform their utility. The currently most reliable product for shelters and community clinics is theintranasal BAP.

What the labels mean

Which vaccines

Core
Considered essential for all animals in that context category, and should be given at intake or as soon as possible. (Guideline definition)
Situational
May have good evidence for use under particular circumstances, but not appropriate for all shelter or community animals. (Guideline definition)

How certain the evidence is

Evidence rating
The task force's own rating of the evidence behind each recommendation, from 0 to 4, recorded when the recommendations were voted. Higher means more certain. A range means the statement rests on more than one voted recommendation and they were rated differently.
Not rated
No rating was recorded for this statement, usually because it was proposed after the vote.

Kinds of evidence

  • ■ Experimental
  • ● Observational
  • ▲ Case report or series
  • ◆ Expert or consensus
  • ■ filled: independent or not stated   □ hollow: manufacturer funded, authored, or supplied
  • (species) study in a different species
  • (model) decision model
  • Italic no study cited: Inference (reasoned from cited claims), Consensus (the task force's judgment), Not assembled (an evidence gap)
