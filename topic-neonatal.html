ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Vaccinating puppies and kittens before 4 weeks of age

The guideline advises starting MLV core vaccines by 4 weeks of age or earlier in shelters, and by 4 to 6 weeks in community clinics depending on risk. This page gathers the claims from the pathogen ledgers that bear on vaccinating puppies and kittens younger than 4 weeks. Each claim also appears on its vaccine page.

The recommendation to vaccinate before 4 weeks in high-risk settings follows Hurley and Newbury (UW Shelter Medicine statement on neonatal puppies, 2023, and 2025 presentations on neonatal puppies and kittens).
