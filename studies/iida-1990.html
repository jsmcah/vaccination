Study's main limitation

What it reports
Mean maternal antibody half-lives were estimated at 13.5 days for CPV, 15.1 days for CDV and 15.4 days for ICHV. 53% (20 of 38) responded to CDV vaccination at 56 days, mainly puppies with titers below 1 to 10.
