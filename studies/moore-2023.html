ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Moore 2023

Full reference
Moore GE, Morrison J, Saito EK, Spofford N, Yang M. 2023. Breed, smaller weight, and multiple injections are associated with increased adverse event reports within three days following canine vaccine administration. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 261(11):1653-1659.
DOI
10.2460/javma.23.03.0181
Design
retrospective cohort study
Species
dog
Animals
4,654,187 dogs (16,087,455 visits)
Population
dogs vaccinated at 1,119 hospitals of a US corporate practice, 2016 to 2020
Funding
independent. No external funding. Moore has served on speakers bureaus for several vaccine makers (acknowledgments)
Study's main limitation
Adverse events from medical records within 3 days, and DA2PP is analyzed as one product.
What it reports
Adverse events followed 0.19% of vaccination visits. The DA2PP rate was 24.6 per 10,000 doses (95% CI 24.2 to 24.9). Rates rose with number of vaccines and smaller body weight.
