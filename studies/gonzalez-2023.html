Gonzalez 2023

Gonzalez SE, Gogal RM, Meindl AG, Boyer N, Nelson S, Everett SE, Vetter CA, Gonzalez JM. 2023. Influence of age and vaccination interval on canine parvovirus, distemper virus, and adenovirus serum antibody titers. Veterinary Immunology and Immunopathology 262:110630.

Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome, and few dogs more than 5 years from vaccination.
What it reports
Protective CDV titers (32 or more) were found in 76% (31 of 41) at more than 3 to 5 years and 62% (95% CI 36 to 82, 8 of 13) beyond 5 years since vaccination. Odds of protection fell 2% per year.
