ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Mila 2018

Full reference
Mila H, Grellet A, Feugier A, Desario C, Decaro N, Buonavoglia C, Mariani C, Chastant-Maillard S. 2018. General and type 2 parvovirus-specific passive immune transfer in puppies, evaluation by early growth. Reproduction in Domestic Animals 53(S3):96-102.
DOI
10.1111/rda.13334
Design
prospective observational cohort (serology, unvaccinated puppies)
Species
dog
Animals
169 puppies from 35 bitches (151 sampled at day 2, 127 to 129 at day 28)
Population
unvaccinated puppies of annually vaccinated bitches in one commercial breeding kennel, followed from birth to 28 days
Funding
not stated. Funded by Royal Canin SAS, a pet food company, and two authors were Royal Canin employees (funding information, affiliations)
Study's main limitation
One breeding kennel, follow-up ends at 28 days, and no vaccine was given.
What it reports
Median CPV maternal HI titer fell from 1:320 at day 2 to 1:40 (IQR 1:20 to 1:80) at day 28. At day 28, 86.8% (112 of 129) of puppies were below 1:160, and 25.8% (39 of 151) were already below 1:160 at day 2.
