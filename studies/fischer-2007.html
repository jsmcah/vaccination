ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Fischer 2007

Full reference
Fischer SM, Quest CM, Dubovi EJ, Davis RD, Tucker SJ, Friary JA, Crawford PC, Ricke TA, Levy JK. 2007. Response of feral cats to vaccination at the time of neutering. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 230(1):52-58.
DOI
10.2460/javma.230.1.52
Design
prospective field serology study (two vaccine arms, no challenge)
Species
cat
Animals
61 feral cats (29 MLV, 32 inactivated FVRCP)
Population
feral cats in a Florida TNR program, vaccinated at neutering and recaptured about 10 weeks later
Funding
not stated. No funding statement in the paper. Vaccines used were Fort Dodge products (footnotes), with no statement that they were donated
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome only, and a substantial share of cats had pre-existing antibody.
What it reports
After one dose, 28 of 29 (97%) MLV cats and 55 of 61 (90%) overall had protective FPV titers about 10 weeks later. The proportion protected did not differ significantly between MLV and inactivated groups (P = 0.2), though MLV gave higher median titers.
