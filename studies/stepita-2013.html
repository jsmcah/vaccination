ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Stepita 2013

Full reference
Stepita ME, Bain MJ, Kass PH. 2013. Frequency of CPV infection in vaccinated puppies that attended puppy socialization classes. Journal of the American Animal Hospital Association 49(2):95-100.
DOI
10.5326/JAAHA-MS-5825
Design
retrospective observational study (clinic records and trainer records)
Species
dog
Animals
1012 clinic puppies plus 231 puppies from trainer classes
Population
puppies 16 weeks or younger vaccinated at least once by a veterinarian, seen at 21 clinics in four US cities, and puppies attending classes with 24 trainers in the same cities
Funding
not stated. No funding statement found. Authors at the University of California, Davis
Study's main limitation
Few class attendees in the clinic sample (48), so the confidence interval around no added risk is wide, and CPV may have been underdiagnosed.
What it reports
15 of 1012 clinic puppies (1.48%, 95% CI 0.83% to 2.43%) were diagnosed with CPV, all at clinics in low-income zip codes. None of the 48 puppies that attended classes was diagnosed (OR 0, 95% CI 0 to 5.60, P = 0.94). None of 279 class attendees overall was suspected of or diagnosed with CPV.

Claims citing this study

  • Puppies that had at least one CPV dose had no added risk from socialization classes MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
