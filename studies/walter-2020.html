Walter 2020

Full reference
Walter J, Foley P, Yason C, Vanderstichel R, Muckle A. 2020. Prevalence of feline herpesvirus-1, feline calicivirus, Chlamydia felis, and Bordetella bronchiseptica in a population of shelter cats on Prince Edward Island. Canadian Journal of Veterinary Research 84(3):181-188.
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey (PCR, virus isolation and culture in cats with URTD, not serology)
Species
cat
Animals
82 cats
Population
shelter cats with URTD signs, PEI Humane Society, Canada
Funding
independent. Atlantic Veterinary College Companion Animal Trust Fund (acknowledgments)
Study's main limitation
Only sick cats were sampled and only 4 were Bordetella positive.
What it reports
4 of 82 (4.9%, 95% CI 1.3% to 12.0%) were Bordetella positive by culture, 3 with FHV-1. All 4 isolates were resistant to cefovecin.
