What it reports
A dog inadvertently given an IN Bb/CPiV vaccine SQ developed a local injection site reaction and acute nonseptic hepatocellular degeneration and necrosis. It was treated with IV fluids and amikacin. Two months later bile acids and histopathology still showed hepatocellular disease.
