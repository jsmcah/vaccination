What it reports
After challenge 2 to 7 weeks post series, leptospirosis developed in 0% of vaccinates for Canicola, Icterohaemorrhagiae and Grippotyphosa and 5% for Pomona, against 78% to 94% of placebo dogs. Urinary shedding was prevented in 100% (Canicola, Grippotyphosa), 89% (Icterohaemorrhagiae) and 95% (Pomona) of vaccinates. The 15-month Grippotyphosa data are those of Grosenbaugh 2018.
