Larson 2007

Full reference
Larson LJ, Schultz RD. 2007. Three-year duration of immunity in dogs vaccinated with a canarypox-vectored recombinant canine distemper virus vaccine. Veterinary Therapeutics 8(2):101-106.
Design
controlled serology study (duration of antibody)
Species
dog
Animals
58 beagles and 239 client-owned dogs
Population
research beagles in a CDV-free facility and client-owned dogs
Funding
mixed. Publication funded by Merial, maker of the vaccine (footnote)
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome, not challenge, and not about onset in shelters.
What it reports
Antibody after recombinant CDV vaccination persisted at least 36 months. Two of 239 client-owned dogs never responded and stayed seronegative.
