ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Elanco 2020

Full reference
Elanco US Inc. Summary of studies supporting USDA product licensure. TruCan Ultra CIV H3N2/H3N8, Canine Influenza Vaccine, H3N2 and H3N8, Killed Virus (product code 15Q5.20). Compiled June 25, 2025. Efficacy approvals July 6, 2020, safety approval November 20, 2024.
Design
experimental challenge study (licensure summary, with field safety study)
Species
dog
Animals
38 puppies in the H3N2 challenge (20 vaccinates, 18 placebo), 37 in the H3N8 challenge, and 688 dogs in the field safety study
Population
puppies 7 weeks old (challenge), and dogs of which 244 of 688 were 7 weeks old or younger (safety)
Funding
manufacturer. Elanco licensure data
Study's main limitation
Unpublished manufacturer summary with lung lesion outcome only and no unvaccinated arm in the safety study.
What it reports
Two SQ doses 3 weeks apart, challenge 3 weeks after dose 2. Median lung consolidation was 0% in vaccinates versus 4% in H3N2 placebo dogs. In the safety study 155 of 688 dogs had at least one adverse event (350 in total), most often diarrhea (4.8%).

Claims citing this study

  • Two doses of H3N2 vaccine reduced signs, shedding and lung lesions on challenge 1 to 3 weeks after the second dose H3N2 canine influenza
  • CIV vaccines have adverse event rates no higher than other canine vaccines H3N2 canine influenza
  • Two doses 3 to 4 weeks apart are needed, starting after 8 weeks of age H3N2 canine influenza
