ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Rong 2014

Full reference
Rong S, Lowery D, Floyd-Hawkins K, King V. 2014. Characterization of an avirulent FCV strain with a broad serum cross-neutralization profile and protection against challenge of a highly virulent vs feline calicivirus. Virus Research 188:60-67.
DOI
10.1016/j.virusres.2014.03.007
Design
experimental challenge study (randomized complete block, 4 vaccine groups vs unvaccinated control, heterologous VS-FCV 33585 challenge, plus in vitro cross-neutralization panels)
Species
cat
Animals
48 cats in the efficacy study (9 unvaccinated, 9 F9 4-way, 10 per FCV 21 group), plus 24 cats in the challenge dose titration and SPF cats used to raise antisera
Population
domestic shorthair cats about 8 weeks old
Funding
manufacturer. All authors at Zoetis Vet Med R&D, Kalamazoo (author affiliations). Vaccines were experimental Zoetis products. No funding statement
Study's main limitation
About 10 cats per group, and Kruskal-Wallis and Fisher tests are named but no p values are reported. Cats were housed by treatment group, and only one VS-FCV challenge strain was used.
What it reports
Cats got two SQ doses 21 days apart of experimental modified-live vaccines and were challenged with VS-FCV 33585 on day 42. Mortality was 78% (7/9) in unvaccinated cats, 44% (4/9) with F9 4-way, and 0% (0/10) in each FCV 21 group (FCV 21 4-way, FCV 21 plus F9 4-way, FCV 21 plus F9 3-way). Median clinical scores were 24, 12, 2.5, 1.5 and 2. Sera from FCV 21 vaccinates neutralized 58.3% to 61.8% of a 26-strain panel versus 40.4% for F9. On the 1993 Michigan panel, F9 antiserum neutralized more isolates than FCV 21 antiserum (42% to 69% vs 36% to 53%).
