Study's main limitation
Single case. Primary infection at the time of the dog's vaccination cannot be fully excluded, as the authors note.
What it reports
Two weeks after her dog received IN live attenuated Bb vaccine (Nobivac KC, strain B-C2), the owner developed bronchitis with malaise and mild fever. Sputum grew Bb, and core genome MLST showed only 3 allelic differences from the vaccine batch strain. She improved after 14 days of trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole. Authors describe it as the first confirmed dog-to-human transmission of a live attenuated Bb vaccine strain.
