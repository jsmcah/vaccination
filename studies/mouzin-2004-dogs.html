Mouzin 2004 (dogs)

Full reference
Mouzin DE, Lorenzen MJ, Haworth JD, King VL. 2004. Duration of serologic response to five viral antigens in dogs. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 224(1):55-60.
DOI
10.2460/javma.2004.224.55
Design
serologic survey (pre- and post-revaccination titers, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
322 dogs
Population
healthy client-owned dogs 2 years or older with a documented series, revaccinated with the same vaccine line
Funding
manufacturer. Authors at Pfizer Animal Health and the vaccine line was Pfizer's (affiliation, product footnote)
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome, 5 to 7 day post-vaccination sample, and manufacturer authorship.
What it reports
316 of 322 (98.1%) dogs either had a CDV titer of 1:32 or more or a 4-fold rise after revaccination, across intervals up to and beyond 48 months.
