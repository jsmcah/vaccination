Sonrier 2000

Full reference
Sonrier C, Branger C, Michel V, Ruvoën-Clouet N, Ganière JP, André-Fontaine G. 2000. Evidence of cross-protection within Leptospira interrogans in an experimental model. Vaccine 19(1):86-94.
DOI
10.1016/S0264-410X(00)00129-8
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
gerbil
Animals
groups of about 4 to 6 gerbils per arm across several assays
Population
laboratory-bred Mongolian gerbils, 2 to 3 months old, immunized with leptospiral extracts and challenged
Funding
mixed. French Ministry of Agriculture and Virbac Laboratory (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Rodent model with experimental extracts, not commercial dog vaccines
What it reports
Whole-cell extracts protected completely against homologous challenge and partly against heterologous challenge. LPS fractions protected only against homologous challenge
