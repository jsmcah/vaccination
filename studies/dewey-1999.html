ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Dewey 1999

Full reference
Dewey CE, Wilson S, Buck P, Leyenaar JK. 1999. The reproductive performance of sows after PRRS vaccination depends on stage of gestation. Preventive Veterinary Medicine 40(3-4):233-241.
DOI
10.1016/S0167-5877(99)00025-2
Design
retrospective observational before-after study of herd records
Species
pig
Animals
47 sow herds (PigCHAMP records, 62,672 sows in the record base)
Population
commercial sow herds in Ontario, Manitoba and the midwestern USA that whole-herd vaccinated with MLV PRRS vaccine
Funding
manufacturer. Boehringer Ingelheim (Canada) Ltd (maker of the MLV PRRS vaccine) and the Ontario Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Historical before-after comparison, confounded by concurrent PRRS outbreaks in some herds, in a different species and virus.
What it reports
Sows vaccinated at any stage of gestation had fewer pigs born alive and weaned and more stillborn and mummified pigs than sows farrowing before vaccine use, with the largest association in the last four weeks of gestation. Authors concluded the MLV vaccine should be given only to non-gestating sows.
