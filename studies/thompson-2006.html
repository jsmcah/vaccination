Study's main limitation
Letter with summary data only. Methods, follow-up of the low-titer puppies and the vaccine are not described.
What it reports
At 14 weeks, 93% had moderate or high HI titers and 257 (7%, 95% CI 6% to 8%) had titers of 32 or less. Of 129 low-titer puppies resampled after a further dose, mostly at 15 or 16 weeks, 121 had high titers, 7 moderate and 1 still low. That puppy responded after a dose at 18 weeks.

Claims citing this study

  • A few puppies remain blocked by maternal antibody past 16 weeks MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Most puppies have responded by 12 to 16 weeks, and late non-responders respond to one more dose MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
