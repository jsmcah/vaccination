ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Jas 2015

Full reference
Jas D, Frances-Duvert V, Vernes D, Guigal PM, Poulet H. 2015. Three-year duration of immunity for feline herpesvirus and calicivirus evaluated in a controlled vaccination-challenge laboratory trial. Veterinary Microbiology 177(1-2):123-131.
DOI
10.1016/j.vetmic.2015.03.009
Design
experimental challenge study (duration of immunity, vaccinate vs control, heterologous FCV challenge 3 years after the 1-year booster)
Species
cat
Animals
25 SPF cats enrolled (15 vaccinated, 5 original controls plus 5 controls added 4 months before challenge). FCV analysis included 13 vaccinated and 10 controls.
Population
SPF cats 10 to 11 weeks old at first vaccination, kept in a confined facility and challenged on day 1506
Funding
manufacturer. Authors are Merial employees, and Merial makes the vaccine tested (Purevax RCPCh FeLV) (affiliations, conflict of interest statement)
Study's main limitation
Single inactivated product at minimum dose, small groups, manufacturer authorship, and controls of mixed age at challenge.
What it reports
Cats got two SQ doses of a non-adjuvanted vaccine with inactivated FCV (G1 and 431) 28 days apart and a booster at 1 year. Three years later, 2 of 13 vaccinates and 6 of 10 controls had severe calicivirosis (oral ulcers with systemic signs, P = 0.04). Median clinical score was 5.0 vs 14.5 (64% reduction in average score). All cats shed FCV, with no difference in shedding between groups.
