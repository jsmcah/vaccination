ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Hofmann-Lehmann 2007

Full reference
Hofmann-Lehmann R, Cattori V, Tandon R, Boretti FS, Meli ML, Riond B, Pepin AC, Willi B, Ossent P, Lutz H. 2007. Vaccination against the feline leukaemia virus: outcome and response categories and long-term follow-up. Vaccine 25(30):5531-5539.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2006.12.022
Design
experimental challenge study (two vaccine studies with long-term follow-up)
Species
cat
Animals
45 SPF cats (study 1, 9 vaccinated and 6 controls; study 2, 10 per group for whole virus, canarypox and control)
Population
SPF cats challenged intraperitoneally at 10 months (study 1) or 4.5 months (study 2), followed up to 13 years
Funding
independent. University of Zurich, Swiss National Science Foundation professorship, Janggen-Poehn Foundation and Roche Research Foundation (acknowledgements). Vaccines were commercial products
Study's main limitation
Small groups, SPF cats and intraperitoneal challenge.
What it reports
Recombinant vaccine PF 87% (p = 0.02), canarypox PF 78% (p = 0.006) and whole virus PF 44% (not significant). All study 2 cats became provirus positive after challenge, two protected cats later reactivated and developed lymphoma, and vaccinated cats in study 1 lived longer (mean 11.3 versus 5.7 years).

Claims citing this study

  • No FeLV vaccine gives complete protection, and vaccinated cats can still become regressively infected FeLV
  • Vaccination protected most challenged cats from progressive infection and prolonged survival FeLV
