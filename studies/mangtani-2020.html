ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Mangtani 2020

Full reference
Mangtani P, Evans SJW, Lange B, Oberle D, Smith J, Drechsel-Baeuerle U, Keller-Stanislawski B. 2020. Safety profile of rubella vaccine administered to pregnant women: a systematic review of pregnancy related adverse events following immunisation, including congenital rubella syndrome and congenital rubella infection in the foetus or infant. Vaccine 38(5):963-978.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2019.11.070
Design
systematic review and meta-analysis
Species
human
Animals
4 cohort studies with unvaccinated comparison, 15 cohorts of susceptible vaccinated pregnant women, 9 other cohorts and case series
Population
women inadvertently given rubella-containing vaccine in or shortly before pregnancy
Funding
independent. Part funded by a small grant from the WHO Global Advisory Committee on Vaccine Safety (funding statement). Authors declare no competing interests
Study's main limitation
Different species and virus, with few and small cohorts having an unvaccinated comparison group.
What it reports
No case of vaccine-associated congenital rubella syndrome was found. The maximum theoretical risk was 1 in 1008 vaccinated susceptible women (upper 95% CI 0.099%), and asymptomatic neonatal vaccine-virus infection occurred in 1.74% (95% CI 1.21 to 2.28).
