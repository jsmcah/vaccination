ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Hurley 2004

Full reference
Hurley KF, Pesavento PA, Pedersen NC, Poland AM, Wilson E, Foley JE. 2004. An outbreak of virulent systemic feline calicivirus disease. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 224(2):241-249.
DOI
10.2460/javma.2004.224.241
Design
retrospective observational study (outbreak investigation with case definitions, univariate risk factor analysis, viral sequencing and serology)
Species
cat
Animals
54 cases (32 confirmed, 16 suspect, 6 possible)
Population
cats at 3 veterinary practices and a rescue organization with foster homes in west Los Angeles, June to August 2002
Funding
independent. Maddie's Fund, the Winn Feline Foundation and the UC Davis Center for Companion Animal Health (title page footnote)
Study's main limitation
Single outbreak with an unknown denominator for most exposures, and few cats known to be unvaccinated, so vaccine effect could not be assessed.
What it reports
Case fatality was 41% (22 of 54), 59% in cats over 1 year and 14% in kittens under 6 months. Adults were more likely to have severe disease or die (OR 9.56, 95% CI 2.82 to 32.39). 35 of 54 (65%) had severe signs. The attack rate in cats hospitalized 12 hours or more with an infected cat was 94% (47 of 50). 23 infected cats were known to be vaccinated, and serum from F9-vaccinated SPF cats did not neutralize FCV-Kaos. Shedding was seen up to 16 weeks after infection.
