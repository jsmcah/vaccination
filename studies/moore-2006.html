Moore 2006

Full reference
Moore GE, Guptill LF, Glickman NW, Caldanaro RJ, Aucoin D, Glickman LT. 2006. Canine leptospirosis, United States, 2002-2004. Emerging Infectious Diseases 12(3):501-503.
DOI
10.3201/eid1203.050809
Design
laboratory database serosurvey
Species
dog
Animals
23,005 dogs tested by MAT
Population
dogs with suspected leptospirosis whose sera were sent to Antech, a national reference laboratory, 2002 to 2004
Funding
independent. CDC grant (acknowledgement). Two authors at VCA Antech, the laboratory
Study's main limitation
Submissions from dogs suspected of leptospirosis, with no clinical confirmation and frequent MAT cross reaction between serovars.
What it reports
The proportion of positive MATs rose from 2002 to 2004 at every cutoff, and was highest for serovars Autumnalis and Grippotyphosa. Autumnalis and Pomona titers correlated most strongly (r = 0.72).
