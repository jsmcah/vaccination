ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Foley 2002

Full reference
Foley JE, Rand C, Bannasch MJ, Norris CR, Milan J. 2002. Molecular epidemiology of feline bordetellosis in two animal shelters in California, USA. Preventive Veterinary Medicine 54(2):141-156.
DOI
10.1016/S0167-5877(02)00022-3
Design
case series (prospective outbreak investigation in two shelters, culture and PFGE)
Species
cat and dog
Population
cats and dogs in a 40-cat, 40-dog no-kill shelter and a large traditional shelter in California
Funding
independent. Maddie's Fund, the Geraldine R. Dodge Foundation and the UC Center for Companion Animal Health (acknowledgements in stored snapshot)
Study's main limitation
Two shelters, and nearly all isolates matched a vaccine strain used in these shelters.
What it reports
Feline culture prevalence was 47% when dogs had kennel cough and 10% three months later when none did. In the second shelter it was 47% in cats and 23% in dogs at the peak. All isolates but one matched the pattern of a vaccine used in these shelters. Feline isolates were more often antibiotic resistant than canine isolates.
