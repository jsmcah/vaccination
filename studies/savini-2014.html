ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Savini 2014

Full reference
Savini G, Lorusso A, Paladini C, Migliaccio P, Di Gennaro A, Di Provvido A, Scacchia M, Monaco F. 2014. Bluetongue serotype 2 and 9 modified live vaccine viruses as causative agents of abortion in livestock: a retrospective analysis in Italy. Transboundary and Emerging Diseases 61(1):69-74.
DOI
10.1111/tbed.12004
Design
retrospective laboratory surveillance study
Species
sheep, cattle, goats, buffalo
Animals
1264 fetuses (663 ovine, 429 bovine, 155 caprine, 17 buffalo)
Population
aborted or malformed fetuses submitted in Italy, from dams vaccinated with BTV-2 or BTV-2 and BTV-9 MLV (Onderstepoort)
Funding
not stated. No funding statement. Authors at the OIE Reference Laboratory for Bluetongue, Istituto G. Caporale, Teramo
Study's main limitation
Retrospective submissions of aborted fetuses with no denominator of vaccinated pregnancies, in different species and virus.
What it reports
BTV vaccine strains were isolated from 31 fetuses (2.4%, 95% CI 1.7 to 3.4), 3.6% of ovine, 1.4% of bovine and 0.6% of caprine fetuses, mostly serotype 9.
