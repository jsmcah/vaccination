ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Shams 2022

Full reference
Shams F, Pourtaghi H, Abdolmaleki Z. 2022. The first evaluation of the effectiveness of canine vaccination schedule by two commercial vaccines in Iran. BMC Veterinary Research 18:119.
DOI
10.1186/s12917-022-03219-2
Design
controlled serology study (randomized, two vaccine arms and saline control, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
24 terrier puppies (8 per group)
Population
healthy puppies from two Iranian breeding farms, dams vaccinated 5 months before whelping, vaccinated at 8, 12 and 16 weeks
Funding
independent. Funded by the authors, no competing interests declared (declarations)
Study's main limitation
Eight puppies per group with antibody outcomes only.
What it reports
In the 8 unvaccinated controls, maternal HI titers at 18 weeks were 1:40 in 6, 1:80 in 1 and 1:160 in 1, so 2 of 8 still had titers of 1:80 or more. Biocan DHPPi+L and Duramune Max 5 both raised titers, with Duramune puppies responding after the first dose and Biocan puppies after the second.
