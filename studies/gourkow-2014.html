ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Gourkow 2014

Full reference
Gourkow N, Hamon SC, Phillips CJC. 2014. Effect of gentle stroking and vocalization on behaviour, mucosal immunity and upper respiratory disease in anxious shelter cats. Preventive Veterinary Medicine 117(1):266-275.
DOI
10.1016/j.prevetmed.2014.06.005
Design
experimental study (alternate-allocation trial of gentling)
Species
cat
Animals
139 cats
Population
cats rated anxious at admission to a British Columbia SPCA shelter
Funding
independent. A non-industry donor and the Morris Animal Foundation (acknowledgements). Authors declare no conflicts
Study's main limitation
Pathogen shedding was pooled across five pathogens, and allocation was alternate rather than random.
What it reports
Gentled cats had higher fecal S-IgA. Shedding rose over 10 days in controls (23%, 35%, 52%) but not in gentled cats, and controls were 2.4 times (95% CI 1.35 to 4.15) more likely to develop URI.
