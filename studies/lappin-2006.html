ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Lappin 2006

Full reference
Lappin MR, Sebring RW, Porter M, Radecki SJ, Veir J. 2006. Effects of a single dose of an intranasal feline herpesvirus 1, calicivirus, and panleukopenia vaccine on clinical signs and virus shedding after challenge with virulent feline herpesvirus 1. Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery 8(3):158-163.
DOI
10.1016/j.jfms.2005.12.001
Design
experimental challenge study (IN vaccine)
Species
cat
Animals
40 kittens (3 vaccinated groups of 10, 10 controls)
Population
unvaccinated kittens given one IN dose 6, 4 or 2 days before FHV challenge
Funding
manufacturer. Funded by a grant from Heska, and one author was at Heska (acknowledgement, affiliation)
Study's main limitation
IN vaccine only, manufacturer funded, and the 2-day group did not differ significantly on its own.
What it reports
Grouped vaccinates had lower clinical scores and temperatures than controls. The 6-day and 4-day groups had significantly lower scores, and the 6-day group shed less FHV on day 6.
