ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Cocker 1986

Full reference
Cocker FM, Newby TJ, Gaskell RM, Evans PA, Gaskell CJ, Stokes CR, Harbour DA, Bourne FJ. 1986. Responses of cats to nasal vaccination with a live, modified feline herpesvirus type 1. Research in Veterinary Science 41(3):323-330.
DOI
10.1016/S0034-5288(18)30624-6
Design
experimental challenge study (IN vaccine)
Species
cat
Animals
32 cats in groups of 4 to 8
Population
SPF cats 3 to 4 months old
Funding
independent. Wellcome Trust grant (acknowledgements). Vaccine was Katavac-CH (Duphar)
Study's main limitation
Four cats per group in the main trials, and IN vaccine only.
What it reports
IN vaccination 2 days before challenge gave partial protection (mean clinical score 26.9 vs 82) and 4 days before gave near complete protection (4.2 vs 86.5). Shedding duration did not differ significantly at 2 days.
