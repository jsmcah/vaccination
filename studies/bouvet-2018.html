Bouvet 2018

Full reference
Bouvet J, Cariou C, Poulard A, Oberli F, Cupillard L, Guigal PM. 2018. Compatibility between a rabies vaccine and a combined vaccine against canine distemper, adenovirosis, parvovirosis, parainfluenza virus and leptospirosis. Veterinary Immunology and Immunopathology 205:93-96.
DOI

Design
controlled serology study (vaccine compatibility, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
42 (28 given the combination vaccine)
Population
SPF beagle puppies 7 to 9 weeks old, seronegative, vaccinated on days 0 and 28
Funding
manufacturer. Authors are employees of Boehringer Ingelheim, maker of both vaccines (conflict of interest statement)
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome in SPF pups, designed to test rabies vaccine interference rather than onset.
What it reports
Mean CAV-2 neutralizing titer rose from about 0.5 to about 3 log10 by day 14 after the first dose (Figure 2). One injection induced high titers against CDV, CAV and CPV in all vaccinated dogs.
