ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Duenwald 1971

Full reference
Duenwald JC, Holland JM, Gorham JR, Ott RL. 1971. Feline panleukopenia: experimental cerebellar hypoplasia produced in neonatal ferrets with live virus vaccine. Research in Veterinary Science 12(4):394-396.
DOI
10.1016/S0034-5288(18)34175-4
Design
experimental study
Species
ferret
Animals
18 female ferrets (15 vaccinated IP at stages from 3 days before breeding to 30 to 42 days after, 3 unvaccinated) plus 16 neonatal kits from 3 litters vaccinated IP at under 24 h, 24 to 48 h, or 48 to 72 h old
Population
pregnant jills and neonatal kits
Funding
government. Washington State University and USDA Agricultural Research Service (affiliation, ARS scientific paper footnote). Vaccine obtained commercially, maker not named
Study's main limitation
Different species, intraperitoneal dosing, and small litters per group.
What it reports
No effect on litter size or ataxia in kits of jills vaccinated at any stage of gestation. Severe cerebellar hypoplasia followed IP MLV FPV vaccine in kits 1 and 2 days old, but not 3 days old.

Claims citing this study

  • Evidence from other species on MLV in pregnancy is indirect and mixed MLV FVRCP
  • MLV FPV vaccine caused cerebellar hypoplasia in 1 to 2 day old ferrets, but not when given later or in pregnancy MLV FVRCP
