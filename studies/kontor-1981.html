Kontor 1981

Full reference
Kontor EJ, Wegrzyn RJ, Goodnow RA. 1981. Canine infectious tracheobronchitis. Effects of an intranasal live canine parainfluenza-Bordetella bronchiseptica vaccine on viral shedding and clinical tracheobronchitis (kennel cough). American Journal of Veterinary Research 42(10):1694-1698.
Design
experimental challenge study (parenteral and intranasal arms, each with controls)
Species
dog
Animals
74 dogs (32 in the parenteral arm with 7 controls, 27 in the intranasal arm with 7 controls, 15 in backpassage studies)
Population
seronegative mixed-breed dogs 4 to 10 weeks old
Funding
manufacturer. Authors at Burns-Biotec Laboratories (affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Arms were challenged differently (CPIV alone after parenteral, CPIV plus Bordetella after intranasal), so the route comparison is indirect.
What it reports
Intranasal vaccine reduced clinical tracheobronchitis by 96% after combined challenge 14 days later. CPIV was shed on 70% of days by controls, 50% after two parenteral doses and 1% after one intranasal dose. Controls given CPIV alone coughed 1.1 days, Bordetella alone 9.7 days and both 18.6 days.
