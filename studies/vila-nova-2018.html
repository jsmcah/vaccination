Study's main limitation

What it reports
Of puppies started at 6 weeks, 80% had protective CDV titers 3 weeks after the first dose and all after the second. In adults unvaccinated for 3 or more years, average time to seroreversion for CDV was estimated at 2.86 years.

Claims citing this study

  • CDV antibody persists for years in most vaccinated dogs, but a minority lose it MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Puppies in low-risk homes can be vaccinated every 3 to 4 weeks MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
