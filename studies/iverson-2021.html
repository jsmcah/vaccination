ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Iverson 2021

Full reference
Iverson SA, Levy C, Yaglom HD, Venkat HL, Artus A, Galloway R, et al. 2021. Clinical, diagnostic, and epidemiological features of a community-wide outbreak of canine leptospirosis in a low-prevalence region (Maricopa County, Arizona). Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 258(6):616-629.
DOI
10.2460/javma.258.6.616
Design
outbreak investigation with case-control study
Species
dog
Animals
71 case dogs (54 confirmed, 17 probable) and 281 control dogs. 44 cases entered the case-control analysis
Population
dogs in Maricopa County, Arizona, January 2016 to June 2017
Funding
independent. No external funding (statement)
Study's main limitation
Confirmed cases included an MAT titer of 1:400 or more in unvaccinated dogs (1:800 if vaccinated) as well as PCR. Controls were recruited by owner survey, and exposures were owner recalled.
What it reports
Two outbreaks: a household cluster of 8 dogs with serovar Djasiman (L. kirschneri) and a community outbreak of 63 dogs with serovar Canicola (L. interrogans). Case dogs were 7.7 times (95% CI 3.5 to 16.7) as likely as controls to have attended dog daycare, and 2.9 times (1.3 to 6.6) as likely to have been boarded overnight, in the 30 days before illness.
