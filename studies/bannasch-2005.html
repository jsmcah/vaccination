Bannasch 2005

Full reference

Design
observational case-control study
Species
cat
Animals

Population

Funding

Study's main limitation
Virus isolation cannot separate new infection from reactivation, and shelters differed widely.
What it reports
FHV prevalence was 3% to 38% by shelter. FHV was associated with URI (OR 2.15, 95% CI 1.42 to 3.24). FHV recovery was lowest in recent arrivals and highest after 6 to 12 days in the shelter.
