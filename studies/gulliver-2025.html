What it reports
Signs began 9 to 23 days after at least one on-label dose of a quadrivalent vaccine with live CDV. Sequencing found a Rockborn-like strain 99.9% identical across four cases and a vaccine vial. Post-vaccinal distemper was confirmed in six cases and presumed in two.
