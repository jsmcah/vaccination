What it reports
Peak titer is expected within 28 days of first vaccination, when the animal can be considered immunized. A booster is due 1 year later regardless of age. Shelters should ensure adopted animals are vaccinated, and vaccine may be given under veterinary supervision in shelters before release. Biting animals are observed 10 days without vaccination. Never-vaccinated exposed dogs and cats are euthanized or quarantined 4 months.
