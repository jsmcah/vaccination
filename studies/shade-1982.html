ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Shade 1982

Full reference
Shade FJ, Rapp VJ. 1982. Studies of a bacterin incorporating an extracted Bordetella bronchiseptica antigen for controlling canine bordetellosis. Veterinary Medicine and Small Animal Clinician 77(11):1635-1639.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
41 vaccinated dogs challenged, plus 444 dogs vaccinated in the field for safety
Population
dogs given an extracted Bb antigen bacterin SQ or IM (control numbers not in the record)
Funding
not stated. Not in the Zotero record. The task force describes it as an industry study.
Study's main limitation
Abstract only. Control group size and challenge timing are not in the record, and the product was an experimental extract.
What it reports
Two doses 2 weeks apart of an extracted antigen bacterin, given SQ or IM, were followed by aerosol challenge. Kennel cough developed in 19% of vaccinated dogs and 60% of unvaccinated controls. Serology showed a response after each dose, and no significant adverse reactions were reported in 444 field-vaccinated dogs.
