ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Pardo 1997

Full reference
Pardo MC, Bauman JE, Mackowiak M. 1997. Protection of dogs against canine distemper by vaccination with a canarypox virus recombinant expressing canine distemper virus fusion and hemagglutinin glycoproteins. American Journal of Veterinary Research 58(8):833-836.
DOI
10.2460/ajvr.1997.58.08.833
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
33 puppies (26 vaccinated, 7 controls)
Population
SPF Beagle puppies 7 to 10 weeks old
Funding
manufacturer. Authors at Rhône Mérieux (affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Two doses 21 days apart, so no single-dose data.
What it reports
Two doses of recombinant CDV combination vaccine, at 40 times less than the commercial dose, protected all 26 pups against virulent challenge 21 days after the booster. All controls became ill.

Claims citing this study

  • Recombinant CDV vaccine protected puppies against challenge, including puppies with maternal antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
