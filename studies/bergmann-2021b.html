ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Bergmann 2021b

Full reference
Bergmann M, Freisl M, Zablotski Y, Khan MAA, Speck S, Truyen U, Hartmann K. 2021. Prevalence of neutralizing antibodies to canine distemper virus and response to vaccination in client-owned adult healthy dogs. Viruses 13(5):945.
DOI
10.3390/v13050945
Design
prospective field serology study (single vaccination, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
97 dogs
Population
healthy client-owned adult dogs in Germany not vaccinated in the previous year
Funding
independent. No external funding. An MSD vaccine was used but MSD had no role, and one author has given talks for MSD (funding and conflict statements)
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome, and almost all dogs already had antibody, so it says little about naive adults.
What it reports
92 of 97 (94.8%, 95% CI 88.2 to 98.1) had antibody before vaccination and none had a 4-fold rise by day 28. Five dogs had no antibody before or after vaccination (humoral non-responders). Age under 2 years was associated with lacking antibody.

Claims citing this study

  • A few adult dogs make no antibody to CDV vaccine, even after repeated doses MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Most previously vaccinated adult dogs already have CDV antibody, and a booster adds little MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
