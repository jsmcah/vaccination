Study's main limitation
Short narrative review that largely restates other reviews, including Spiri 2022, and gives no pooled prevalence data.
What it reports
Calls FCV a prevalent and impactful pathogen with high mutability. It says a subset of cats seems resistant to infection despite continuous exposure, likely from immune-mediated or genetic factors. It also says vaccination lessens clinical signs but does not prevent infection or carriage, and that shelters with high turnover show more FCV genetic variability.
