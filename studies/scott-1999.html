ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Scott 1999

Full reference
Scott FW, Geissinger CM. 1999. Long-term immunity in cats vaccinated with an inactivated trivalent vaccine. American Journal of Veterinary Research 60(5):652-658.
Design
experimental long-term challenge study (vaccinated vs unvaccinated contact controls)
Species
cat
Animals
17 cats challenged (9 vaccinated, 8 unvaccinated controls), from an original 15 vaccinated and 17 controls
Population
SPF cats vaccinated at 8 and 12 weeks with an inactivated adjuvanted FPV-FHV-FCV vaccine (Fel-O-Vax PCT, Fort Dodge) and kept in isolation
Funding
independent. Private donations to the Cornell Feline Health Center (title page footnote)
Study's main limitation
Small sample of SPF cats in isolation, and only one inactivated product.
What it reports
Vaccinated cats kept high FPV titers and had solid protection against virulent FPV challenge 7.5 years after vaccination. Protection against FHV and FCV was partial (relative efficacy 52% and 63%).
