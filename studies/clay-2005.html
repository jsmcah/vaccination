Study's main limitation
Small laboratory study of one vaccine strain on human office surfaces, with no cats or shelters.
What it reports
FCV survived up to 48 to 72 hours on telephone buttons and receivers, 24 to 48 hours on a computer mouse and telephone wire, and 8 to 12 hours on keyboard keys and brass. A 90% reduction in titer took under 4 hours on most surfaces.
