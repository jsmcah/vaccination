ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Patterson 2007

Full reference
Patterson EV, Reese MJ, Tucker SJ, Dubovi EJ, Crawford PC, Levy JK. 2007. Effect of vaccination on parvovirus antigen testing in kittens. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 230(3):359-363.
DOI
10.2460/javma.230.3.359
Design
prospective controlled study (8 vaccine arms, no challenge)
Species
cat
Animals
64 SPF kittens (8 groups of 8)
Population
SPF kittens 8 to 10 weeks old, single dose of 1 of 8 commercial MLV or inactivated FPV vaccines, SQ or IN
Funding
mixed. Supported by the Harold H. Morris Trust Fund and the Merck-Merial Veterinary Scholars Program. Vaccines and test kits donated by IDEXX, Synbiotics, AGEN, Fort Dodge, Heska, Intervet, Pfizer and Schering-Plough (title page footnote)
Study's main limitation
SPF kittens rather than shelter cats, antibody measured only at day 14, and the primary outcome was fecal antigen test interference.
What it reports
At 14 days after one dose, 85% of kittens given MLV vaccines had protective FPV titers versus 31% given inactivated vaccines. Vaccine-associated positive fecal parvovirus antigen tests were uncommon and varied by test.
