ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Bouvet 2020

Full reference
Bouvet J, Lemaitre L, Cariou C, Scotto M, Blain C, Oberli F, Cupillard L, Guigal PM. 2020. A canine vaccine against Leptospira serovars Icterohaemorrhagiae, Canicola and Grippotyphosa provides cross protection against Leptospira serovar Copenhageni. Veterinary Immunology and Immunopathology 219:109985.
DOI
10.1016/j.vetimm.2019.109985
Design
randomized challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
14 (7 vaccinated, 7 controls)
Population
Beagle puppies 8 to 9 weeks old, seronegative to 9 serovars, given Eurican DAPPi-Lmulti (Canicola, Icterohaemorrhagiae, Grippotyphosa) or Eurican DAPPi without lepto on days 0 and 28
Funding
manufacturer. All authors employed by Boehringer Ingelheim, maker of the vaccine (competing interest statement)
Study's main limitation
Seven dogs per group, intraperitoneal challenge only 2 weeks after the second dose, non-blinded clinical examination, and all authors from the manufacturer.
What it reports
After challenge with serovar Copenhageni (serogroup Icterohaemorrhagiae, not itself in the vaccine), 4 of 7 controls were euthanized and 5 of 7 showed clinical signs, and all 7 had renal infection and kidney lesions. No vaccinated dog showed signs, leptospiremia, leptospiruria, renal carriage or kidney lesions.
