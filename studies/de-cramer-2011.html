ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

De Cramer 2011

Full reference
De Cramer KGM, Stylianides E, van Vuuren M. 2011. Efficacy of vaccination at 4 and 6 weeks in the control of canine parvovirus. Veterinary Microbiology 149(1):126-132.
DOI
10.1016/j.vetmic.2010.11.004
Design
prospective field serology study (vaccinated and control groups, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
121 puppies (86 vaccinated at 4 weeks, 33 controls listed, 27 controls analysed)
Population
German shepherd, Rottweiler and Boerboel puppies in three South African breeding kennels, dams vaccinated within 6 months before whelping
Funding
manufacturer. Merial supplied vaccines and transponders and covered laboratory expenses (acknowledgement). Vaccines were Merial Primodog and Eurican
Study's main limitation
Field kennels with likely natural exposure, so some titer rises may reflect infection rather than vaccine. Manufacturer support.
What it reports
Maternal HI titers at 4 weeks ranged from 64 to 2048 (mean 256). After a high-titer monovalent MLV CPV dose at 4 weeks, 80% (69 of 86) seroconverted, against 2 of 27 unvaccinated controls. All Rottweiler non-responders had seroconverted by the second dose.

Claims citing this study

  • Many puppies respond to MLV CPV vaccine at 4 to 6 weeks despite maternal antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
