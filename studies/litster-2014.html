ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Litster 2014

Full reference
Litster A, Benjanirut C. 2014. Case series of feline panleukopenia virus in an animal shelter. Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery 16(4):346-353.
DOI
10.1177/1098612X13497738
Design
retrospective case series
Species
cat
Animals
145 cats (66 in-contact, 27 FPV survivors, 52 FPV non-survivors)
Population
cats and kittens in one adoption-guarantee shelter in Chicago (PAWS Chicago), January to October 2010, mostly under 6 months old
Funding
independent. Maddie's Fund grant (funding statement). Authors declare no conflicts
Study's main limitation
Single shelter, descriptive records review with no comparison of vaccination protocols.
What it reports
Cases peaked over summer but occurred year-round. 52 of 79 clinically affected cats died. Cats were vaccinated with MLV within 24 h of intake, and some kittens developed FPV after vaccination.

Claims citing this study

  • Shelter outbreaks are common and deadly, and the cats affected are mostly unvaccinated or incompletely vaccinated MLV FVRCP
  • FPV kills 50% to 90% of affected cats MLV FVRCP
  • Kittens too young to vaccinate at admission die of FPV in shelters MLV FVRCP
