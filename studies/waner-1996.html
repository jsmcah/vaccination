ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Waner 1996

Full reference
Waner T, Naveh A, Wudovsky I, Carmichael LE. 1996. Assessment of maternal antibody decay and response to canine parvovirus vaccination using a clinic-based enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay. Journal of Veterinary Diagnostic Investigation 8(4):427-432.
DOI
10.1177/104063879600800404
Design
controlled serology study (no challenge, no unvaccinated group)
Species
dog
Animals
38 pups (4 from each of 10 litters, 2 died early) of 10 vaccinated beagle bitches
Population
purpose-bred beagle pups of bitches vaccinated in mid-gestation, vaccinated with MLV CPV at 6 and 9 weeks
Funding
mixed. Two authors were employed by Biogal, maker of the ELISA kit evaluated. Vaccines were Intervet Nobivac products, with no statement on funding (affiliations and sources list)
Study's main limitation
Single colony of beagles with antibody outcomes only, and the vaccination schedule after 9 weeks is described inconsistently.
What it reports
By HI, vaccination succeeded in 18 of 38 pups (47%) at 6 weeks, 19 (50%) at 9 weeks, and 1 pup only at 16 weeks. Maternal antibody half-life was 11.6 days, and HI titers of 1:80 or more interfered with vaccination.

Claims citing this study

  • A few puppies remain blocked by maternal antibody past 16 weeks MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Many puppies respond to MLV CPV vaccine at 4 to 6 weeks despite maternal antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
