Thibault JC, Bouvet J, Cupillard L, Cariou C, Oberli F. 2022. Compatibility between a rabies vaccine and two canine combined vaccines against canine distemper, adenovirosis, parvovirosis, parainfluenza virus disease and leptospirosis, with or without canine coronavirus. Comparative Immunology, Microbiology and Infectious Diseases 86:101803.

What it reports
All puppies given either combination vaccine seroconverted to CPIV, with similar antibody kinetics with or without concurrent rabies vaccine.
