Study's main limitation
All included studies at high risk of bias in at least one domain. Challenge models use high intraperitoneal doses
What it reports
Pooled relative risk 0.16 for clinical disease and 0.12 for renal carriage in vaccinated versus control dogs, summarized as 84% and 88% protection. Protection against Canicola, Australis and Grippotyphosa inconsistent for some brands
