ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Coutts 1996

Full reference
Coutts AJ, Dawson S, Binns S, Hart CA, Gaskell CJ, Gaskell RM. 1996. Studies on natural transmission of Bordetella bronchiseptica in cats. Veterinary Microbiology 48(1-2):19-27.
DOI
10.1016/0378-1135(95)00128-X
Design
experimental study (queen to kitten transmission under barrier conditions, then intranasal inoculation of kittens, no vaccine)
Species
cat
Animals
2 queens and 9 kittens
Population
SPF cats from a colony endemically infected with Bordetella, Liverpool UK
Funding
mixed. Two authors funded by Intervet UK, one by the BSAVA Clinical Studies Trust Fund, plus the Whitley Animal Protection Trust (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Two litters, so the transmission findings are anecdotal.
What it reports
Both queens began shedding Bordetella after parturition (9 days and 6 weeks), but no kitten became infected or seroconverted before weaning. After intranasal inoculation at 15.5 weeks, kittens showed mild upper respiratory signs and shed the organism for 19 weeks, when the study ended. Doxycycline did not end shedding in two kittens.
