ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Carmichael 1983

Full reference
Carmichael LE, Joubert JC, Pollock RVH. 1983. A modified live canine parvovirus vaccine. II. Immune response. The Cornell Veterinarian 73(1):13-29.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
Laboratory groups of 4 to 138 dogs (13 in the minimum dose study, 13 in duration of immunity groups, 138 seronegative pups in the efficacy test, 12 in the maternal antibody comparison, 6 by the oronasal route, 29 in the combined vaccine study), plus 724 paired field sera from about 5,865 doses given in 13 kennels and practices
Population
Mostly seronegative beagles from the Baker Institute colony, plus field pups under about 4 months old in research kennels and private practices
Funding
independent. American Kennel Club, Seeing Eye Foundation (Morris Animal Foundation) and Ralston Purina Co (acknowledgement). The attenuated strain was the authors' own experimental Cornell vaccine, not a commercial product
Study's main limitation
Small laboratory groups with an experimental vaccine strain, and challenge was done only at 12 and 24 months, not in the first days after vaccination.
What it reports
In seronegative dogs HI antibody was detected in most by day 3 and in all by day 4 after one SC dose, with protective titers (HI 40 to 80) usually within 4 days. Efficacy in 138 seronegative pups was over 99% by sequential test. Vaccinated dogs resisted challenge at 12 months (7 dogs) and 24 months (6 dogs) without shedding. In field pups 98% (183 of 187) with prevaccination HI under 10 responded, about 50% at HI near 20, and none above 80. Oronasal vaccination did not overcome maternal antibody.

Claims citing this study

  • One MLV dose produces protective antibody in seronegative dogs within about 4 days MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Oronasal attenuated CPV immunized pups, including pups dosed at 4 days old MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Dogs vaccinated once resisted challenge 12 and 24 months later MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
