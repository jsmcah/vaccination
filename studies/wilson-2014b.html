ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Wilson 2014b

Full reference
Wilson S, Illambas J, Siedek E, Stirling C, Thomas A, Plevová E, Sture G, Salt J. 2014. Vaccination of dogs with canine parvovirus type 2b (CPV-2b) induces neutralising antibody responses to CPV-2a and CPV-2c. Vaccine 32(42):5420-5424.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2014.07.102
Design
controlled serology study
Species
dog
Animals
22 beagles in two studies (15 in a maternal antibody study, 7 in a duration of immunity study)
Population
6-week-old beagle pups of defined serological status, vaccinated twice 3 weeks apart
Funding
manufacturer. Authors from Zoetis and Bioveta, and the vaccine is produced and marketed by Zoetis (author affiliations and contributions statement)
Study's main limitation
Cross-protection is inferred from antibody cross-reactivity, because challenge was with CPV-2b only.
What it reports
After two doses of a CPV-2b multivalent vaccine, sera neutralized CPV-2a, CPV-2b and CPV-2c at titers higher than against CPV-2, and anamnestic responses followed CPV-2b challenge at 5 weeks or 1 year. The authors infer cross-protection against 2a and 2c.

Claims citing this study

  • Current CPV-2 and CPV-2b vaccines protect against the CPV-2a, 2b and 2c field variants MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
