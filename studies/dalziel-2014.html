Study's main limitation
Models H3N8, not H3N2. The vaccine argument is made from demographics without modeling it.
What it reports
H3N8 had sporadic short-lived outbreaks with endemic hotspots in large shelters (mean R0 3.9) acting as refugia. States that shelter turnover is too high for an inactivated vaccine to work, given an expected residence time of about 10 days. Control strategies with efficacies of 65% or less would reduce prevalence but not always eradicate the virus.
