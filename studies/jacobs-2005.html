ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Jacobs 2005

Full reference
Jacobs AAC, Theelen RPH, Jaspers R, Horspool LJI, Sutton D, Bergman JGHE, Paul G. 2005. Protection of dogs for 13 months against Bordetella bronchiseptica and canine parainfluenza virus with a modified live vaccine. Veterinary Record 157(1):19-23.
DOI
10.1136/vr.157.1.19
Design
experimental challenge study (long-term)
Species
dog
Animals
18 SPF beagles (12 vaccinated, 6 controls) plus 6 10-week-old challenge controls
Population
SPF beagle puppies vaccinated intranasally once at 3 weeks of age (Nobivac KC)
Funding
manufacturer. All authors at Intervet International or Intervet UK, maker of the vaccine (affiliations)
Study's main limitation
One replicate with groups housed separately, small groups, and manufacturer authorship.
What it reports
After combined Bordetella and CPIV challenge 56 weeks after vaccination, clinical scores were 61% lower than same-age controls and 90% lower than 10-week-old controls. Controls shed CPIV for about a week and vaccinates almost none.
