ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Reese 2008

Full reference
Reese MJ, Patterson EV, Tucker SJ, Dubovi EJ, Davis RD, Crawford PC, Levy JK. 2008. Effects of anesthesia and surgery on serologic responses to vaccination in kittens. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 233(1):116-121.
DOI
10.2460/javma.233.1.116
Design
controlled serology study
Species
cat
Animals
32 SPF kittens
Population
SPF kittens neutered at 7, 8 or 9 weeks or not neutered, rabies vaccine at 14 weeks
Funding
mixed. Supported in part by the Merck Merial Scholars Program
Study's main limitation
Rabies vaccine was given at 14 weeks, after surgery, so it does not test rabies vaccination at the time of surgery.
What it reports
9% of kittens lacked adequate rabies titers at 17 weeks, 3 weeks after the inactivated dose. Neutering near the first MLV dose did not impair responses.

  • More than 97% of cats reach adequate antibody after one rabies dose Rabies (killed or recombinant)
