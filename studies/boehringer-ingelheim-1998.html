ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Boehringer Ingelheim 1998

Full reference
Boehringer Ingelheim Animal Health USA Inc. Summary of studies supporting USDA product licensure, 124 46J9.23 (Canine Imuno-Vax 10, Canine Spectra 10, Solo-Jec 10). Compiled 2 June 2020. Studies approved March and April 1998.
Design
experimental challenge study (manufacturer licensing summary)
Species
dog
Animals
17 (11 vaccinates, 6 controls) for CAV-1, 22 (11 and 11) for CAV-2
Population
6-week-old puppies seronegative to the challenge virus, two SC doses 21 days apart
Funding
manufacturer. Licensing data submitted by the maker (document)
Study's main limitation
Licensing summary with no methods detail, and challenge was 21 days after the second dose.
What it reports
After ICH challenge no vaccinate died, against 6 of 6 controls, though 3 of 11 vaccinates had serous ocular discharge. In the CAV-2 study vaccinates had milder signs and brief low-titer nasal virus compared with controls.
