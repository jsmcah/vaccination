ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Lehr 2008

Full reference
Lehr C, Jayappa H, Erskine J, Brown A, Sweeney D, Wasmoen T. 2008. Demonstration of 1-year duration of immunity for attenuated Bordetella bronchiseptica vaccines in dogs. Veterinary Therapeutics 9(4):257-262.
Design
experimental challenge study (randomized, unvaccinated controls, masking not stated)
Species
dog
Animals
44 puppies (14 monovalent IN Bb, 15 trivalent IN Bb/CPiV/CAV-2, 15 unvaccinated controls)
Population
healthy puppies 6 to 7 weeks old with no vaccination history for Bb, CPiV or CAV-2 and low antibody titers, kept in isolation for the year
Funding
manufacturer. Sponsored by Intervet/Schering-Plough Animal Health, all authors are its employees, and both vaccines tested (Intrac, Intra-Trac 3) are its products (sponsorship footnote and affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Sponsor-run study with no statement on observer masking, and dogs kept in isolation for a year, which tests duration in unexposed dogs rather than in a shelter population.
What it reports
After IN challenge on days 371 and 372, total mean cough scores were 2.9 (monovalent), 2.6 (trivalent) and 11.7 (controls) (P <= 0.0001), and mean days coughing were about 2 versus 9. Of controls 93% coughed, while 4 of 14 monovalent and 3 of 15 trivalent dogs never coughed and no vaccinate scored 3. At day 21 after challenge all controls were still shedding versus 21% of monovalent and 0% of trivalent dogs (P < 0.0001). Serum Bb titers stayed above controls for the full year.
