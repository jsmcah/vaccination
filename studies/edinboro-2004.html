Edinboro 2004

Full reference
Edinboro CH, Ward MP, Glickman LT. 2004. A placebo-controlled trial of two intranasal vaccines to prevent tracheobronchitis (kennel cough) in dogs entering a humane shelter. Preventive Veterinary Medicine 62(2):89-99.
DOI

Design

Species
dog
Animals

Population

Funding
mixed. Not funded as a study. Fort Dodge and Schering-Plough donated the vaccines, and a fellowship supported the first author (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Low cough incidence left the trial unable to detect differences, and staff were not blinded.
What it reports
Cough within 30 days was 10.7% (95% CI 7.2 to 14.2) with IN-BP, 10.2% (7.0 to 13.4) with IN-BPA and 13.5% (9.7 to 17.3) with placebo (P = 0.37). Days in the shelter was the strongest risk factor (3% per day). No difference in adverse events.
