Study's main limitation
Convenience sample likely enriched for households with respiratory disease, and few household pets (41).
What it reports
Bordetella was isolated from 11% (82/740), 19.5% (36/185) in rescue catteries and 0 of 41 household pets. Households with dogs with recent respiratory disease were associated with feline isolation (OR 13.70, 95% CI 5.01 to 38.67), but not within rescue catteries (OR 1.20, 95% CI 0.46 to 3.13). Current respiratory disease was associated with isolation only in rescue cattery cats.
