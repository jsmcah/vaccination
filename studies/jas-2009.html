ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Jas 2009

Full reference
Jas D, Aeberle C, Lacombe V, Guiot AL, Poulet H. 2009. Onset of immunity in kittens after vaccination with a non-adjuvanted vaccine against feline panleucopenia, feline calicivirus and feline herpesvirus. The Veterinary Journal 182(1):86-93.
DOI
10.1016/j.tvjl.2008.05.025
Design
experimental challenge study (vaccinate vs control)
Species
cat
Animals
FPV trials 22 SPF kittens (1-week challenge 5 vaccinated, 5 controls, 3-week challenge 6 vaccinated, 6 controls), plus separate FHV and FCV trials
Population
SPF kittens 8 to 9 weeks old
Funding
manufacturer. All authors except one consultant are Merial employees, and Merial markets the vaccine tested (Purevax RCPCh-FeLV) (conflict of interest statement, affiliations)
Study's main limitation
SPF kittens under laboratory challenge, small groups, and manufacturer authorship.
What it reports
One SQ dose of MLV FPV protected all vaccinated kittens against IP FPV challenge at 1 week (0/5 deaths vs 3/5 controls) and at 3 weeks (0/6 vs 4/6), with no leukopenia in vaccinates.
