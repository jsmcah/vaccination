ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Shams 2023

Full reference
Shams F, Pourtaghi H. 2023. Effect of maternally derived antibodies on two commercial vaccines in changes of serum antibody titres against distemper in puppies. Veterinary Medicine and Science 9(2):698-703.
DOI
10.1002/vms3.1076
Design
controlled serology study (randomized, blinded, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
24 puppies (8 per group)
Population
puppies in Iran vaccinated at 8, 12 and 16 weeks with one of two vaccines or saline
Funding
not stated. Authors declare no conflict of interest
Study's main limitation
Small groups and antibody outcome. The Questions doc found the results hard to follow.
What it reports
Maternal antibody in all control puppies fell below protective levels before 14 weeks. In both vaccinated groups all puppies reached protective titers after the second dose.

Claims citing this study

  • Maternal CDV antibody halves about every 8 days and usually wanes by 10 to 14 weeks MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Puppies in low-risk homes can be vaccinated every 3 to 4 weeks MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
