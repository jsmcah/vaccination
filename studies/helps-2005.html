What it reports
Bordetella was detected by PCR in 5.0% of cats in catteries with URTD and 1.3% without (OR 3.50, 95% CI 1.64 to 7.46). Seroprevalence was 61% and 41%, and 77% in rescue shelters. Less than excellent hygiene and age were associated with seropositivity. Dog presence was associated with seropositivity only in univariable analysis.
