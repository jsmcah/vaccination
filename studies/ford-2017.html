ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Ford 2017

Full reference
Ford J, McEndaffer L, Renshaw R, Molesan A, Kelly K. 2017. Parvovirus infection is associated with myocarditis and myocardial fibrosis in young dogs. Veterinary Pathology 54(6):964-971.
DOI
10.1177/0300985817725387
Design
retrospective observational case-control study (archived pathology tissues)
Species
dog
Animals
40 cases and 41 controls
Population
dogs under 2 years old necropsied or biopsied at Cornell 2007 to 2015, cases with myocardial necrosis, inflammation or fibrosis and age-matched controls without myocardial lesions
Funding
independent. NIH training grant T35OD010941 and the AVMA-AVMF 2nd Opportunity Research Scholarship for student support. Authors declare no conflicts
Study's main limitation
Referral pathology archive with small numbers, so it shows association with myocardial lesions rather than how often infected puppies develop myocarditis.
What it reports
CPV-2 DNA was found in the myocardium of 12 of 40 cases (30%, 95% CI 18% to 45%) and 2 of 41 controls (5%, 95% CI 0.1% to 16%), P = .003. Infected dogs had a median age of 61 days. The introduction cites up to 10% mortality in unvaccinated adults and 91% in puppies.
