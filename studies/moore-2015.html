ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Moore 2015

Full reference
Moore MC, Davis RD, Kang Q, Vahl CI, Wallace RM, Hanlon CA, Mosier DA. 2015. Comparison of anamnestic responses to rabies vaccination in dogs and cats with current and out-of-date vaccination status. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 246(2):205-211.
DOI
10.2460/javma.246.2.205
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey (paired samples)
Species
dog and cat
Animals
74 dogs and 33 cats
Population
dogs and cats presented for rabies booster or after exposure
Funding
not stated. Part of an MPH thesis at Kansas State University
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome, convenience sample.
What it reports
All animals had 0.5 IU/mL or more 5 to 15 days after the booster. Out-of-date dogs had larger fold increases than current dogs.
