What it reports
C. felis is most often detected in cats 2 to 12 months old, and kittens under 2 months may be protected by maternal antibody. Elementary bodies survive only a few days in the environment, so transmission is mainly direct contact and infection is most common in multi-cat households and breeding catteries. Co-infections are common and may increase severity. Recommends 4 weeks of doxycycline and treating all cats in the household.
