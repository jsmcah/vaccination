What it reports
Describes FCV as highly mutagenic, with evolution rates among the highest identified for RNA viruses. Persistence is attributed to accumulating mutations, sequential reinfection and recombination, and the review says there seems to be sufficient genetic relatedness between strains for some cross-protection through vaccination. It gives VS-FCV morbidity and mortality of up to 67% and calls limping syndrome rather uncommon.
