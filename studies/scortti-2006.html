ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Scortti 2006

Full reference
Scortti M, Prieto C, Martinez-Lobo FJ, Simarro I, Castro JM. 2006. Effects of two commercial European modified-live vaccines against porcine reproductive and respiratory syndrome viruses in pregnant gilts. The Veterinary Journal 172(3):506-514.
DOI
10.1016/j.tvjl.2005.07.015
Design
experimental study (4 arms)
Species
pig
Animals
16 pregnant gilts (4 per group)
Population
gilts at 90 days of gestation, intranasal exposure to control, field strain or one of two MLV vaccine strains
Funding
independent. Spanish CICYT grant AGF99-0403, plus a Spanish International Cooperation Agency fellowship (acknowledgements). Vaccine strains were from Hipra and Syva products
Study's main limitation
Four gilts per group, late-gestation exposure only, in a different species and virus.
What it reports
Both MLV strains caused viremia and crossed the placenta (3/4 and 2/4 gilts), but congenital infection had no marked detrimental effect on piglet performance compared with controls.
