Study's main limitation
Surveillance summary with no case definition details or facility descriptions, and missing exposure data for many cases.
What it reports
201 cases (184 lab confirmed) and 13 deaths, serovar Canicola, attributed to dog-to-dog transmission. Of cases with data, 101 of 154 (66%) had daycare or boarding exposure, with two facility clusters of more than 30 dogs, and 51 of 105 visited dog parks. The county advised treating lepto vaccine as core for dogs there.
