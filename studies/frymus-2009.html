Study's main limitation

What it reports
Cats respond better than dogs, with more than 97% reaching 0.5 IU/mL after the first vaccination. Kittens should be vaccinated at 12 to 16 weeks and revaccinated 1 year later. Rabies vaccines are considered safe even in neonatal kittens.
