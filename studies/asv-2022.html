What it reports
Recommends MLV core vaccines in shelters, including for pregnant animals. It calls cerebellar hypoplasia a theoretical complication of MLV panleukopenia vaccination of pregnant cats, generally outweighed in shelters by the risk of abortion and maternal and kitten death from panleukopenia.
