ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Hainer 2021

Full reference
Hainer N, Velineni S, Bowers A, Waite C, Walker J, Wilmes L, Tague A, King V, Millership J, Martorell S. 2021. Oral vaccination of dogs with a monovalent live-avirulent vaccine confers 1 year of immunity against Bordetella bronchiseptica challenge. The Veterinary Journal 278:105775.
DOI
10.1016/j.tvjl.2021.105775
Design
experimental challenge study (randomized, placebo controlled, masked)
Species
dog
Animals
40 dogs randomized (20 oral Bb vaccine, 20 sterile water), 38 analyzed (19 per group)
Population
antibody profile defined beagle puppies 7 to 9 weeks old, Bb seronegative and culture negative, from vaccinated dams
Funding
manufacturer. All authors employed by Zoetis, maker of the vaccine (Versican Plus Bb Oral, Vanguard B Oral), and the study was approved by the Zoetis IACUC (conflict of interest statement and author affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Laboratory challenge of one product with a feline-origin challenge strain, 19 dogs per group, and groups housed separately for the full year before challenge.
What it reports
A single oral dose at minimum manufacturing dose, with aerosol challenge on day 366, reduced cough on 2 or more consecutive days from 17 of 19 controls (89.5%) to 3 of 19 vaccinates (15.8%, P = 0.0011). Duration of cough was 8.6 days (95% CI 5.6 to 11.5) versus 1.5 days (95% CI -1.5 to 4.5), P < 0.0001. Bb shedding occurred in 19 of 19 controls and 5 of 19 vaccinates, with duration 11.9 versus 0.6 days. Any cough occurred in 18 of 19 controls and 12 of 19 vaccinates, and no vaccine side effects were seen.
