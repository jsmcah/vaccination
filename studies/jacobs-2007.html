ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Jacobs 2007

Full reference
Jacobs AAC, Bergman JGHE, Theelen RPH, Jaspers R, Helps JM, Horspool LJI, Paul G. 2007. Compatibility of a bivalent modified-live vaccine against Bordetella bronchiseptica and CPiV, and a trivalent modified-live vaccine against CPV, CDV and CAV-2. Veterinary Record 160(2):41-45.
DOI
10.1136/vr.160.2.41
Design
experimental challenge study (three arms)
Species
dog
Animals
24 puppies (3 groups of 8)
Population
puppies given intranasal Bb and CPIV with or without injectable DAP, or DAP only
Funding
not stated. Abstract only. First author and most coauthors are the Intervet authors of Jacobs 2005
Study's main limitation
The comparison arm received no CPIV vaccine, and only the abstract was available.
What it reports
After combined challenge 3 weeks later, clinical scores in the two intranasal groups were nearly 90% lower than in the DAP-only group, with lower CPIV yields. Intranasal vaccine did not interfere with DAP antibody responses.
