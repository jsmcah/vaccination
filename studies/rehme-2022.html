Rehme 2022

Full reference
Rehme T, Hartmann K, Truyen U, Zablotski Y, Bergmann M. 2022. Feline panleukopenia outbreaks and risk factors in cats in animal shelters. Viruses 14(6):1248.
DOI
10.3390/v14061248
Design
observational cross-sectional outbreak study with risk factor analysis
Species
cat
Animals
150 cats in 4 shelters
Population
cats in 4 Bavarian shelters (3 with ongoing outbreaks, 1 without), sampled June to December 2020
Funding
independent. No external funding. Authors declare no conflicts (funding and COI statements)
Study's main limitation
Outbreak setting with vaccination status from shelter records and passive immunization common among cases.
What it reports
42 of 150 cats (28.0%) had panleukopenia and 73 (48.7%) shed parvovirus. Disease was more likely in unvaccinated cats (p < 0.001). Of the 42 cases, 10 had no immunization, 30 had hyperimmune serum only, and 2 had MLV vaccine. The three outbreak shelters recorded 76 deaths.
