ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Lappin 2019

Full reference
Lappin MR, Stasiak K. 2019. Clinical effects induced by 1 dose of H3N2 vaccine in a 7 day challenge model (abstract ID20). 2019 ACVIM Forum Research Abstract Program. Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine 33(5):2495-2496.
DOI
10.1111/jvim.15597
Design
experimental challenge study (conference abstract)
Species
dog
Animals
20 beagles (8 vaccinated, 8 unvaccinated, 4 inoculated seeders)
Population
young adult spayed female beagles negative for H3N2 by PCR and serology
Funding
manufacturer. Second author at Zoetis, and the vaccine was a Zoetis monovalent H3N2 product (author affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Abstract only, 8 dogs per arm, and clinical scores only with no serology or virology.
What it reports
One SQ dose on day -7, contact exposure to inoculated dogs on day 0. Scores did not differ over days 1 to 7, but vaccinates had lower total and respiratory scores over days 1 to 14, 1 to 21 and 1 to 28.
