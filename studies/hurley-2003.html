Study's main limitation
Narrative review whose key outbreak figures rest on the authors' unpublished data, later published as Hurley 2004.
What it reports
Reports an attack rate of 94% in one outbreak and VS-FCV mortality approaching 50%, both from unpublished data. States that vaccination has not appeared protective and that neither intranasal nor parenteral vaccination gave significant protection against FCV-Kaos (unpublished data). Also reports that 4 kittens orally immunized with F9 had milder signs after FCV-Ari challenge.
