ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Scott-Garrard 2018

Full reference
Scott-Garrard MM, Chiang Y-W, David F. 2018. Comparative onset of immunity of oral and intranasal vaccines against challenge with Bordetella bronchiseptica. Veterinary Record Open 5:e000285.
DOI
10.1136/vetreco-2018-000285
Design
experimental challenge study (randomized by litter and gender, placebo controlled, observers blinded)
Species
dog
Animals
29 dogs (10 oral Recombitek Oral Bordetella, 10 IN Nobivac Intra-Trac3, 9 oral placebo)
Population
beagles about 8 weeks old, seronegative and tracheal culture negative for Bb before vaccination, challenged by aerosol with two virulent Bb strains 7 days after a single vaccination
Funding
manufacturer. Study sponsored by Merial (now part of Boehringer Ingelheim), maker of the oral vaccine tested, and all authors are Merial employees (funding and competing interests statements)
Study's main limitation
Small groups with a binary case definition (cough on 2 or more consecutive days), so the 95% CI for the prevented fraction runs from 0.67 to 1.00 and the study cannot distinguish oral from IN. Comparator was the sponsor's competitor product and only one challenge timepoint was tested.
What it reports
With challenge 7 days after one dose, 8 of 9 placebo dogs (88.9%) and 0 of 10 in each vaccine group met the case definition (P<0.0001), prevented fraction 1.00 (95% CI 0.67 to 1.00) for both vaccines. Neither vaccine prevented colonization, as all 20 vaccinates and 8 of 9 placebo dogs were tracheal culture positive 14 days after challenge. Consecutive-day serous nasal discharge occurred in 4 of 10 IN dogs, 2 of 9 placebo dogs and 0 of 10 oral dogs. No vaccine adverse reactions were seen.
