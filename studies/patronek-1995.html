Patronek GJ, Glickman LT, Johnson R, Emerick TJ. 1995. Canine distemper infection in pet dogs: II. A case-control study of risk factors during a suspected outbreak in Indiana. Journal of the American Animal Hospital Association 31(3):230-235.

Funding

Study's main limitation
Practice records, with missing vaccine history counted as unvaccinated.
What it reports
Lack of vaccination was associated with a 350-fold increase in risk and 93.8% of cases were attributed to it. Mixed-breed and young dogs were overrepresented.
