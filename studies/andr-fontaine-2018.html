ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

André-Fontaine 2018

Full reference
André-Fontaine G, Triger L. 2018. MAT cross-reactions or vaccine cross-protection: retrospective study of 863 leptospirosis canine cases. Heliyon 4(11):e00869.
DOI
10.1016/j.heliyon.2018.e00869
Design
retrospective observational (laboratory case records)
Species
dog
Animals
727 MAT-positive cases (147 unvaccinated, 580 fully vaccinated) among 3,512 suspected
Population
dogs with suspected leptospirosis submitted to a French veterinary school laboratory over five years
Funding
no specific grant. First author holds leptospirosis patents (competing interest statement)
Study's main limitation
Serogroup assigned by MAT, which cross-reacts, so heterologous infection cannot be confirmed. Vaccinated and unvaccinated cases are compared without a denominator of exposed dogs
What it reports
In unvaccinated cases liver failure occurred in 57.8% and kidney disease in 51.7%. In dogs given bivalent Icterohaemorrhagiae and Canicola bacterins, hepatitis fell to 46.5% and acute kidney injury to 21.6%, including cases whose MAT-prominent serogroup was not in the vaccine

Claims citing this study
