Study's main limitation
Outbreak descriptions without denominators or comparison groups.
What it reports
CAV-1 was confirmed in all four outbreaks, with CDV, CPV-2c or canine coronavirus also found in three. In the first shelter, which did not vaccinate on intake, 11 of 20 sick pups died. One shelter vaccinated all dogs. The imported pups may have been vaccinated in the face of maternal antibody.
