Study's main limitation
Narrative review, so the attribution of Australian outbreaks to low vaccination is the author's interpretation of cited work rather than new data.
What it reports
Attributes the re-emergence of panleukopenia in Australian shelters since 2014 partly to the failure of many shelters to routinely vaccinate cats, alongside kitten dissemination, crowding and poor infection control. Gives mortality of 50% to 80% even with treatment.
