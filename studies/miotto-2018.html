ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Miotto 2018

Full reference
Miotto BA, Guilloux AGA, Tozzi BF, Moreno LZ, da Hora AS, Dias RA, Heinemann MB, Moreno AM, de Souza Filho AF, Lilenbaum W, Hagiwara MK. 2018. Prospective study of canine leptospirosis in shelter and stray dog populations: identification of chronic carriers and different Leptospira species infecting dogs. PLOS ONE 13(7):e0200384.
DOI
10.1371/journal.pone.0200384
Design
cross-sectional study with prospective follow-up of PCR-positive dogs
Species
dog
Animals
123 (24 in shelter 1, 92 in shelter 2, 7 stray dogs)
Population
dogs in two shelters and a stray population in São Paulo state, Brazil
Funding
independent. FAPESP grant and CNPq and CAPES fellowships (funding statement)
Study's main limitation
Two shelters differing in vaccination, crowding, building condition and rodent control at once, so the effect of vaccination cannot be separated from hygiene
What it reports
Shelter 1 (single or small-group housing, frequent cleaning, routine pest control, no lepto vaccination) had 1 of 24 dogs shedding and no MAT titers. Shelter 2 (groups up to 10, leaks, debris, heavy rodent signs, frequent lepto vaccination) had 10 of 92 (10.87%) shedding and 47 (51%) MAT positive, mostly vaccine related. Overall 13 of 123 shed, and 3 were persistent shedders
