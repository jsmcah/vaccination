ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Dall'Ara 2023

Full reference
Dall'Ara P, Lauzi S, Turin L, Servida F, Barbieri L, Zambarbieri J, Mazzotti G, Granatiero F, Scarparo E, Mirabile A, Bo S, Filipe J. 2023. Prevalence of serum antibody titers against core vaccine antigens in Italian cats. Life 13(12):2249.
DOI
10.3390/life13122249
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey
Species
cat
Animals
740 cats (567 owned, 173 stray)
Population
owned and stray (colony or shelter) cats seen at clinics in northern Italy, 435 vaccinated and 305 unvaccinated
Funding
independent. No external funding. Authors declare no conflicts (funding and conflict statements). Test kits were VacciCheck (Biogal)
Study's main limitation
In-clinic semi-quantitative dot ELISA, and FHV antibody is not an accepted marker of protection.
What it reports
Protective titers against FPV, FHV and FCV in 68.1%, 49.1% and 78.6% of cats. FHV protective titers were more common in owned than stray cats (52.6% vs 37.6%, P = 0.0007), and most cats did not keep FHV titers for 3 years after vaccination.
