Study's main limitation
Narrative review, and its vaccination statements rest on older sources, including a 1990 textbook chapter.
What it reports
Gives ICH mortality of 10% to 30% (citing Cabasso). One dose protects without maternal antibody, but schedules need at least two doses 3 to 4 weeks apart from 8 to 10 weeks. Intranasal CAV-2 may overcome maternal antibody but can cause mild respiratory disease. Infections in vaccinated dogs are linked to maternal antibody rather than variants.
