Full reference

DOI
10.2460/ajvr.1996.57.11.1653
Design
experimental study (immunoglobulin kinetics)
Species
cat
Animals

Funding

Study's main limitation
Total immunoglobulin, not FHV-specific antibody.
What it reports
Kittens absorbed IgG and IgA from colostrum, and these declined with short half-lives. Stone 2020 cites it for maternal IgG being lowest at 3 to 4 weeks.
