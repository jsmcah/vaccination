ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Jarrett 1996

Full reference
Jarrett O, Ganiere JP. 1996. Comparative studies of the efficacy of a recombinant feline leukaemia virus vaccine. Veterinary Record 138(1):7-11.
DOI
10.1136/vr.138.1.7
Design
experimental challenge study (three vaccines and controls)
Species
cat
Animals
56 SPF kittens (trial 1, 12 per vaccine and 8 controls; trial 2, 6 vaccinated and 6 controls)
Population
SPF kittens vaccinated twice from 9 weeks, challenged 2 weeks after the second dose
Funding
manufacturer. Supported by a grant from Virbac, maker of Leucogen (acknowledgements). Vaccines bought from a distributor
Study's main limitation
Small groups, intraperitoneal challenge, early challenge after vaccination, and 1990s products.
What it reports
After intraperitoneal FeLV-A challenge, persistent viremia developed in 5 of 12 Leucogen, 10 of 12 Leukocell 2, 12 of 12 Leucat and 7 of 8 control kittens, so only Leucogen protected significantly. Leucogen also protected against oronasal FeLV-ABC challenge (1 of 6 vaccinated versus 5 of 6 controls).
