Study's main limitation
Narrative review in which feline data rest mainly on one cited study (Scott and Geissinger 1999) and the efficacy figure is an unattributed estimate.
What it reports
States that feline core vaccines have a minimum duration of immunity over 3 years and that the FPV vaccine is highly effective (estimated 99%).
