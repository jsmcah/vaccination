ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Bergmann 2020

Full reference
Bergmann M, Speck S, Rieger A, Truyen U, Hartmann K. 2020. Antibody response to feline herpesvirus-1 vaccination in healthy adult cats. Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery 22(4):329-338.
DOI
10.1177/1098612X19845702
Design
prospective field serology study (single vaccination, no challenge)
Species
cat
Animals
110 cats
Population
healthy owned and breeder cats 1 year or older in Germany, not vaccinated in the past 12 months
Funding
manufacturer. Partly funded by Boehringer Ingelheim (funding statement). Vaccine was Purevax RCP (Merial)
Study's main limitation
Antibody only, 28-day follow-up, and neutralizing antibody is a weak marker for FHV protection.
What it reports
40.9% (45 of 110) had FHV antibody before vaccination. Only 8.3% (9 of 109, 95% CI 4.2 to 15.1) had a four-fold rise after vaccination. 37 of 65 seronegative cats had been vaccinated before.

Claims citing this study

  • Previously vaccinated adult cats rarely show an FHV titer rise after a booster MLV FVRCP
