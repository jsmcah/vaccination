ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Reagan 2014

Full reference
Reagan KL, Hawley JR, Lappin MR. 2014. Concurrent administration of an intranasal vaccine containing feline herpesvirus-1 (FHV-1) with a parenteral vaccine containing FHV-1 is superior to parenteral vaccination alone in an acute FHV-1 challenge model. The Veterinary Journal 201(2):202-206.
DOI
10.1016/j.tvjl.2014.05.003
Design
experimental challenge study (IN plus SQ vs SQ vs control)
Species
cat
Animals
24 kittens (3 groups of 8)
Population
FHV-naive 8-week-old purpose-bred kittens, challenged 1 week after one dose
Funding
manufacturer. Funded in part by an unrestricted donation from Zoetis, whose vaccines (Felocell 3, Felocell FVR C) were studied (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Eight kittens per group, one SQ kitten withdrawn, and no IN-only group.
What it reports
IN plus SQ gave lower clinical scores (P = 0.01) and less FHV DNA than SQ alone. SQ alone did not differ from unvaccinated controls in clinical scores. IN plus SQ kittens had more signs before challenge.
