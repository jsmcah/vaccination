ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Gore 2005

Full reference
Gore TC, Lakshmanan N, Duncan KL, Coyne MJ, Lum MA, Sterner FJ. 2005. Three-year duration of immunity in dogs following vaccination against canine adenovirus type-1, canine parvovirus, and canine distemper virus. Veterinary Therapeutics 6(1):5-14.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
41 (23 vaccinates, 18 controls in groups of 6)
Population
seronegative beagles bred for research, vaccinated SC at 7 and 11 weeks, held in isolation for 3 years
Funding
manufacturer. Funded by Intervet, which employed all authors (funding footnote, affiliations)
Study's main limitation
Six controls per challenge and the maker's own product (Continuum DAPP).
What it reports
No vaccinate showed signs after intravenous CAV-1 challenge 37 months after the second dose, while 5 of 6 controls had severe signs and 3 died. CAV-2 SN GMT was 357 at 36 months.
