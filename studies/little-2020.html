What it reports
Reports North American FeLV prevalence of 2.3% to 3.4% in large surveys. For shelters, says testing is optional and FeLV vaccination not recommended for individually housed cats, testing is essential before group housing, and testing plus FeLV vaccination are recommended for cats group housed long term or in sanctuaries. Recommends two doses 3 to 4 weeks apart from 8 weeks, a booster at 1 year, and testing before vaccination.
