What it reports
Reports North American FeLV prevalence of 2.3% to 3.4% in large surveys. For shelters, says testing is optional and FeLV vaccination not recommended for individually housed cats, testing is essential before group housing, and testing plus FeLV vaccination are recommended for cats group housed long term or in sanctuaries. Recommends two doses 3 to 4 weeks apart from 8 weeks, a booster at 1 year, and testing before vaccination.

Claims citing this study

  • Guidelines give two doses 3 to 4 weeks apart, starting as early as 8 weeks FeLV
  • Guidelines advise boosters every 1 to 3 years after the series and a 1-year booster, with the interval set by risk FeLV
  • AAFP 2020 advises testing cats before FeLV vaccination, and the ASV position statement asks shelters to weigh the cost of testing low-risk, individually housed cats. With shelter prevalence near 3%, blanket intake screening finds few positive cats FeLV
  • AAFP 2020 and the ASV position statement advise testing cats before they enter group housing FeLV
