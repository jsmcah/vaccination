ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Cao 2023

Full reference
Cao L, Liu J, Li Y, Xie D, Yan Q, Li Q, Cao Y, Du W, Li J, Ye Z, Zhou D, Kang C, Cao S. 2023. Biological characteristics of feline calicivirus epidemic strains in China and screening of broad-spectrum protective vaccine strains. Vaccines 11(12):1858.
DOI
10.3390/vaccines11121858
Design
experimental challenge study (inactivated bivalent vaccine vs DMEM control, challenge with the two strains contained in the vaccine, plus mouse immunogenicity screen and in vitro cross-neutralization)
Species
cat (mice for strain screening)
Animals
50 cats (15 in the pathogenicity study, 35 in the vaccine study in 7 groups of 5, of which 4 groups or 20 cats were challenged) and 40 mice
Population
British shorthair cats 8 to 12 weeks old, FCV antibody <1:2 and antigen negative, in China
Funding
manufacturer. Ten of 13 authors employed by Wuhan Keqian Biology Co. and one by Zhejiang Hisun Animal Healthcare Products Co. (conflicts of interest). Funded by the Experimental Animal Research Project of Hubei Province (Grant No. 2023CFA005) (funding statement)
Study's main limitation
Challenge was only with the two strains in the vaccine, the FCV-255 vaccine group was not challenged, and cross-neutralization used serum from one cat per group. Five cats per group with results shown mainly in figures.
What it reports
Seven Chinese isolates shared 77.37% to 80.60% whole-genome nucleotide identity with FCV-255. Cats got inactivated FCV-HB7 plus FCV-HB10 (bivalent), boosted at day 21, and were challenged intranasally 21 days after the booster (day 42) with FCV-HB7 or FCV-HB10. Vaccinates showed no obvious clinical signs and no deaths over 14 days, with lower shedding than DMEM controls, which had severe signs and some deaths. One-cat sera showed bivalent neutralization titers of at least 1:148 against all seven strains, while FCV-255 vaccine serum reached 1:32 against one strain and was below 1:4 against the other six.
