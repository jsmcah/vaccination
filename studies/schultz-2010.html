Study's main limitation
Original data are in dogs only, and the feline statements rely on cited work, mainly Scott and Geissinger 1999 and the author's earlier reviews.
What it reports
States that one dose of MLV feline core vaccine given at 16 weeks or older provides long-lasting (many years to a lifetime) immunity in a very high percentage of animals.

Claims citing this study

  • One MLV dose given at 16 weeks or older gives years to lifelong protection MLV FVRCP
  • Immunity after MLV lasts years MLV FVRCP
