Pekkarinen HM, Karkamo VK, Vainio-Siukola KJ, Hautaniemi MK, Kinnunen PM, Gadd TK, Holopainen RH. 2024. Post-vaccinal distemper-like disease in two dog litters with confirmed infection of vaccine virus strain. Comparative Immunology, Microbiology and Infectious Diseases 105:102114.

Design
case series
Species
dog
Animals
two litters (10 and 5 puppies)
Population
pure-bred puppies in Finland (litter A first vaccinated at 11 to 12 weeks)
Funding
manufacturer. No specific grant, but one author is employed by MSD Animal Health (competing interest statement)
Study's main limitation
Two litters, and an inherited immune defect could not be excluded.
What it reports
In litter A, five puppies fell ill a few days after vaccination and four died or were euthanized. In litter B, two of five developed neurologic signs months later. Vaccine-strain (Onderstepoort) RNA was confirmed in lesions.
