ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Arega 2020

Full reference
Arega S, Conan A, Sabeta CT, Crafford JE, Wentzel J, Reininghaus B, Biggs L, Leisewitz AL, Quan M, Toka F, Knobel DL. 2020. Rabies vaccination of 6-week-old puppies born to immunized mothers: a randomized controlled trial in a high-mortality population of owned, free-roaming dogs. Tropical Medicine and Infectious Disease 5(1):45.
DOI
10.3390/tropicalmed5010045
Design
randomized controlled trial (owner blinded, placebo controlled)
Species
dog
Animals
358 puppies randomized (179 vaccine, 179 sterile water placebo)
Population
owned free-roaming puppies born to vaccinated dams in Mpumalanga, South Africa, a high-mortality population
Funding
independent. No external funding (funding statement)
Study's main limitation
Single site with very high background mortality. The sex interaction was a secondary objective. Survival followed only to 13 weeks of age
What it reports
88% (95% CI 80.7% to 93.3%) of puppies vaccinated at 6 weeks had titers of 0.5 IU/mL or more 21 days later. No significant overall effect on all-cause survival (HR 1.35, 95% CI 0.83 to 2.18), but the effect differed by sex (p = 0.02), with HR 3.09 (95% CI 1.24 to 7.69) in females and 0.79 (95% CI 0.41 to 1.53) in males

Claims citing this study

  • Safety evidence for rabies vaccination under 12 weeks is limited. One randomized trial in 6-week-old puppies found no clear overall effect on survival but higher mortality in vaccinated females. The ABCD and ASV guidelines describe it as safe Rabies (killed or recombinant)
