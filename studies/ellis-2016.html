ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Ellis 2016

Full reference
Ellis JA, Gow SP, Waldner CL, Shields S, Wappel S, Bowers A, Lacoste S, Xu Z, Ball E. 2016. Comparative efficacy of intranasal and oral vaccines against Bordetella bronchiseptica in dogs. The Veterinary Journal 212:71-77.
DOI
10.1016/j.tvjl.2016.04.004
Design
experimental challenge study (randomized)
Species
dog
Animals
48 SPF beagle puppies (3 groups)
Population
Bordetella-naive SPF beagle puppies about 8 weeks old
Funding
manufacturer. Zoetis funded the study and five coauthors are Zoetis employees (conflict of interest statement)
Study's main limitation
Bordetella challenge only, so it says nothing about CPIV.
What it reports
After Bordetella challenge 21 days after vaccination, intranasally vaccinated puppies were less sick than controls and than orally vaccinated puppies.
