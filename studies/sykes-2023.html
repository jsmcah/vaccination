Study's main limitation
Consensus, with levels of agreement rather than graded evidence. Its shelter recommendation cites three shedding studies
What it reports
Recommends annual vaccination of all dogs from 12 weeks, and initial and booster doses 3 weeks apart for shelter dogs at intake because subclinical shedding has been documented in shelter dogs. States vaccines give over 80% protection in trials and at least 12 months immunity, that zoonotic risk from sick dogs appears low with basic precautions, and that isolation is not required because leptospires are not easily transmitted between dogs
