ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Toman 2002

Full reference
Toman M, Faldyna M, Knotigova P, Pokorova D, Sinkora J. 2002. Postnatal development of leukocyte subset composition and activity in dogs. Veterinary Immunology and Immunopathology 87(3-4):321-326.
DOI
10.1016/S0165-2427(02)00058-2
Design
experimental study (immune development, includes a vaccination experiment)
Species
dog
Animals
37 beagle pups in the vaccination experiment (5, 12, 13 and 7 vaccinated at 2, 4, 6 and 8 weeks)
Population
beagle pups with low maternal antibody (titers 1:100 or less) vaccinated with a commercial MLV parvovirus vaccine
Funding
independent. Grant Agency of the Czech Republic (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
The vaccination experiment is reported only in one figure and a paragraph, with antibody outcome and no challenge.
What it reports
Pups vaccinated at 2 to 8 weeks all produced antibody. Pups vaccinated at 2 weeks had lower antibody at day 7 than older pups but similar levels at day 14 in most. The 2-week-old pups showed only a 0.5 C temperature rise on day 3 and a fall in CD4 cells 7 to 10 days after vaccination.

Claims citing this study

  • Puppies vaccinated at 1 day or 2 weeks old responded, with no serious harm reported MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
