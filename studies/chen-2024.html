ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Chen 2024

Full reference
Chen J, Long J, Zhou H, Huang C, Zhu Y, Wang R, Zhang H, Qin Y, Ouyang K, Wei Z, Huang W, Chen Y. 2024. Isolation and characterization of Chlamydia felis and its pathogenesis in cats. Veterinary Microbiology 295:110128.
DOI
10.1016/j.vetmic.2024.110128
Design
experimental challenge study (no vaccine arm), with a cross-sectional PCR survey of owned cats
Species
cat
Animals
396 cats surveyed. 13 kittens challenged (5 high dose, 5 low dose, 3 controls)
Population
cats with or without URTD or conjunctivitis from households and pet hospitals in Guangxi, China, March 2022 to August 2023. Challenge kittens 3 to 4 months old
Funding
mixed. Guangxi Natural Science Foundation (2021GXNSFAA220070) and Guangxi Nanning Heyi Biological Technology Co., Ltd., which employs the second author (acknowledgements, affiliations). The paper does not say whether the company makes vaccines
Study's main limitation
Not a shelter population, and the challenge used one isolate in 10 kittens with no vaccine arm.
What it reports
Mycoplasma felis was most prevalent (24.75%), followed by FCV (15.91%), C. felis (11.62%), FHV-1 (5.56%) and feline influenza A (1.52%). Challenged kittens developed severe conjunctivitis with transient fever and respiratory signs and shed C. felis until 53 days. High-dose kittens had interstitial pneumonia. No deaths are reported.
