ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Goodnow 1979

Full reference
Goodnow RA, Shade FJ. 1979. Control of bordetellosis in the dog. Canine Practice 6(4):43-46.
Design
experimental challenge study (three challenge experiments, aerosol challenge, unvaccinated controls)
Species
dog
Animals
42 dogs in 3 groups (Group I 4 vaccinated and 4 controls, Group II 5 vaccinated and 5 controls, Group III 4 vaccinated 4 days before challenge, 12 vaccinated 5 days before challenge and 8 shared controls)
Population
mixed-breed dogs 6 to 12 weeks old, negative for Bb by nasal culture and free of CPIV (SV5) antibody, randomly assigned to groups
Funding
manufacturer. Both authors at Biologics Research and Development, Burns-Biotec Laboratories, Omaha (author affiliation). No funding statement
Study's main limitation
Very small groups with no statistical testing, outcomes described only in brief prose and figures, and the vaccine was an experimental product from the authors' own company. Methods for Groups I and II do not state challenge dose, and the 4-day and 5-day groups share the same 8 controls.
What it reports
With challenge 14 days after one IN dose of live Bb, 1 of 4 vaccinated dogs coughed mildly for 1 day while 4 of 4 controls had harsh productive cough for an average of 8.7 days, and vaccinates had at least 95% fewer Bb in nasal secretions. In Group II, 0 of 5 vaccinates and 5 of 5 controls had cough, Bb lung lesions and excess tracheal mucus at necropsy 7 days after challenge. With challenge at 4 days after vaccination, 3 of 4 vaccinates and 7 of 8 controls were not protected, and at 5 days 2 of 12 vaccinates and 7 of 8 controls developed tracheobronchitis.
