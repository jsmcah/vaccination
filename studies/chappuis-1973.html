ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Chappuis 1973

Full reference
Chappuis G, Terré J. 1973. Immunisation contre la maladie de Carré et la maladie de Rubarth. II. Installation précoce de l'immunité. Recueil de Médecine Vétérinaire 149(2):177-186.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
76 puppies in total, 24 IV and 19 SQ distemper vaccinates, 4 distemper controls
Population
SPF Beagles 3 to 4 months old from seronegative dams, company colony
Funding
manufacturer. Both authors at IFFA-Mérieux, which prepared the vaccine (affiliation)
Study's main limitation
One to four dogs per challenge day, intracerebral challenge, and the company's own vaccine.
What it reports
Immunity to distemper was complete 48 hours after IV vaccination and from day 5 after SQ vaccination. All four controls died after intracerebral Snyder Hill challenge.

Claims citing this study

  • One SQ MLV dose protected SPF puppies against intracerebral challenge from day 5 after vaccination MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
