ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Kennedy 2007

Full reference
Kennedy LJ, Lunt M, Barnes A, McElhinney L, Fooks AR, Baxter DN, Ollier WER. 2007. Factors influencing the antibody response of dogs vaccinated against rabies. Vaccine 25(51):8500-8507.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2007.10.015
Design
retrospective observational (laboratory serology records)
Species
dog
Animals
10,483
Population
dogs tested at the UK Veterinary Laboratories Agency in 2002 for the Pet Travel Scheme
Funding
government. Two authors funded by UK Defra grant SEV3800 (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Laboratory submissions for pet travel, not a random sample. Antibody outcome only. Number of doses and timing not controlled
What it reports
Size, age, breed, sampling interval and vaccine affected pass rates and median titers. Dogs under 1 year had a lower antibody response than adults, and smaller dogs had higher titers than larger breeds
