ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Martella 2005

Full reference
Martella V, Cavalli A, Decaro N, Elia G, Desario C, Campolo M, Bozzo G, Tarsitano E, Buonavoglia C. 2005. Immunogenicity of an intranasally administered modified live canine parvovirus type 2b vaccine in pups with maternally derived antibodies. Clinical and Diagnostic Laboratory Immunology 12(10):1243-1245.
DOI
10.1128/CDLI.12.10.1243-1245.2005
Design
controlled serology study (single arm, intranasal route, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
78 pups from 16 litters
Population
5 to 7 week old pups born to bitches vaccinated with MLV CPV-2 before mating, Italy
Funding
independent. Grant from CEGBA, an Italian academic centre of excellence (acknowledgements). Vaccine was the authors' own laboratory CPV-2b strain
Study's main limitation
Single arm with no parenteral group run alongside, no challenge, and an experimental vaccine strain rather than a licensed product.
What it reports
Intranasal CPV-2b vaccine (strain 29/97, 68th passage, 10^4.5 TCID50) given at 5 weeks and again at 7 weeks if needed. Seroconversion by HI was 100% at MDA titers of 80 or less, 10 of 15 (66.6%) at 160, 6 of 16 (37.5%) at 320 and 0 of 4 at 640. The authors judged intranasal use as effective as parenteral use of the same strain in an earlier study.
