ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Albas 2013

Full reference
Albas A, Picolo MR, Soares CN, Bachega HVU, Tarumoto MH. 2013. Humoral immune response in dogs and cats vaccinated against rabies in southeastern Brazil. Journal of Venomous Animals and Toxins including Tropical Diseases 19:17.
DOI
10.1186/1678-9199-19-17
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey
Species
dog and cat
Animals
834 serum samples
Population
dogs and cats in two Brazilian cities 12 months after the 2009 national vaccination campaign
Funding
independent. FAPESP grant 08/54266-3 (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Vaccination history of sampled animals was not verified, and the campaign vaccine was not described in detail.
What it reports
About 51% to 52% of dogs and 33% to 50% of cats had titers of 0.5 IU/mL or more a year after the campaign.
