Full reference
Wilson S, Siedek E, Thomas A, King V, Stirling C, Plevová E, Salt J, Sture G. 2014. Influence of maternally-derived antibodies in 6-week old dogs for the efficacy of a new vaccine to protect dogs against virulent challenge with canine distemper virus, adenovirus or parvovirus. Trials in Vaccinology 3:107-113.
DOI
10.1016/j.trivac.2014.06.001
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
15 dogs per study (5 MDA-negative vaccinated, 5 MDA-positive vaccinated, 5 MDA-positive controls)
Population
6-week-old puppies with and without maternal antibody
Funding
manufacturer. Authors employed by Zoetis or Bioveta (author statement)
Study's main limitation
Two doses, small groups, manufacturer study.
What it reports
Two doses 3 weeks apart protected puppies against CDV challenge whether or not they had maternal antibody. Maternal-antibody-positive puppies responded only after the second dose.
