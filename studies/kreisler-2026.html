Study's main limitation
One shelter, with recombinant vaccine use clustered in the second outbreak, so the vaccine-type association with infection may be confounded by time.
What it reports
36 of 68 (53%) had detectable antibody at intake. In adults with no vaccine history, 29 of 30 (97%) MLV versus 14 of 24 (58%) recombinant recipients seroconverted (OR 14.2, 95% CI 2.3 to 88.1). All 23 PCR-positive dogs were intact. After accounting for immortal time, a day-14 second dose showed no measurable benefit (HR 2.5, 95% CI 0.8 to 7.8). 11 of 12 MLV first-dose non-responders eventually seroconverted.
