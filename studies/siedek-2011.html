ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Siedek 2011

Full reference
Siedek EM, Schmidt H, Sture GH, Raue R. 2011. Vaccination with canine parvovirus type 2 (CPV-2) protects against challenge with virulent CPV-2b and CPV-2c. Berliner und Münchener Tierärztliche Wochenschrift 124(1-2):58-64.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Population
specific pathogen free beagles vaccinated at 8 to 9 and 11 to 12 weeks of age (abstract)
Funding
not stated. Author affiliations are not in the Zotero record and the PDF could not be checked
Study's main limitation
Only the abstract could be checked. Two-dose protocol, so it does not bear on single-dose protection.
What it reports
Per the abstract, dogs given two doses of the CPV-2 strain NL-35-D vaccine stayed healthy after CPV-2b or CPV-2c challenge 3 weeks after the second dose and shed over 100 times less virus than controls, which all became ill.

Claims citing this study

  • Current CPV-2 and CPV-2b vaccines protect against the CPV-2a, 2b and 2c field variants MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
