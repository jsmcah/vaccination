Study's main limitation
Single litter, and the isolate could not be identified as vaccine-derived or field virus.
What it reports
Two surviving kittens had hydranencephaly with cerebellar agenesis or hypoplasia and parvoviral DNA in brain tissue. The authors attributed this to in utero infection, possibly from vaccination at the junction of the first and second trimesters.
