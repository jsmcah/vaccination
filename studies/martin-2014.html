ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Martin 2014

Full reference
Martin LER, Wiggans KT, Wennogle SA, Curtis K, Chandrashekar R, Lappin MR. 2014. Vaccine-associated Leptospira antibodies in client-owned dogs. Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine 28(3):789-792.
DOI
10.1111/jvim.12337
Design
prospective field serology study (four vaccine arms, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
32
Population
healthy client-owned dogs with no lepto vaccine in the prior year
Funding
independent. Funded by IDEXX Laboratories, a diagnostics company rather than a vaccine maker, with two IDEXX employee authors (author note)
Study's main limitation
Small sample, antibody outcome only
What it reports
Every vaccine induced some MAT titers of 800 or more. Most dogs were antibody negative 1 year later and anamnestic responses varied
