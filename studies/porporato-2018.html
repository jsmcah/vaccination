ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Porporato 2018

Full reference
Porporato F, Horzinek MC, Hofmann-Lehmann R, Ferri F, Gerardi G, Contiero B, Vezzosi T, Rocchi P, Auriemma E, Lutz H, Zini E. 2018. Survival estimates and outcome predictors for shelter cats with feline panleukopenia virus infection. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 253(2):188-195.
DOI
10.2460/javma.253.2.188
Design
retrospective cohort study (outcome predictors among infected cats)
Species
cat
Animals
177 shelter cats with FPV infection
Population
cats from one large shelter in northwestern Italy during an outbreak that became endemic, treated at one veterinary hospital 2011 to 2013
Funding
not stated. No funding statement. Authors declare no conflicts (acknowledgments)
Study's main limitation
Single shelter and hospital, with vaccination status unknown for 20% and too few vaccinated cats to analyze.
What it reports
36 of 177 (20.3%) survived to discharge, so 141 died. Of 142 cats with known status, 2 (1.4%) had been vaccinated.
