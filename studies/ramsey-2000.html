Study's main limitation
Narrative review whose vaccine statement rests on one small experimental study (Wills 1987).
What it reports
Describes C. psittaci (now C. felis) as mainly a conjunctival pathogen spread by direct contact with ocular secretions. Mild infection resolves in 2 to 6 weeks in kittens and under 2 weeks in older cats, and carriers can relapse after stress. Vaccination 4 weeks before challenge reduced conjunctivitis severity but did not prevent or decrease shedding. Doxycycline for 21 days stopped shedding within 6 days. Still recommends vaccination for semiprotective immunity.
