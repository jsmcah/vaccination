ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Jas 2012

Full reference
Jas D, Coupier C, Edlund Toulemonde C, Guigal PM, Poulet H. 2012. Three-year duration of immunity in cats vaccinated with a canarypox-vectored recombinant rabies virus vaccine. Vaccine 30(49):6991-6996.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2012.09.068
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
cat
Animals
27 vaccinated and 11 control cats challenged
Population
seronegative laboratory cats vaccinated at 12 weeks and boosted 1 year later
Funding
manufacturer. All authors Merial employees, study approved by the Merial Ethical Committee
Study's main limitation
Manufacturer study of one product. The 27 cats with the lowest titers were chosen for challenge.
What it reports
No vaccinated cat developed rabies after challenge 3 years after the booster, while all 11 controls died or were euthanized with signs.
