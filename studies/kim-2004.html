ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Kim 2004

Full reference
Kim D, Ahn SJ, Jeoung SY, Hwang JW, Pak SI. 2004. Serological response of puppies to the selected canine vaccines and vaccination schedules against canine distemper virus. Journal of Veterinary Clinics 21(2):87-92.
Design
controlled serology study (randomized, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
120 puppies (20 per group), plus 7 followed for maternal antibody
Population
healthy puppies in Korea assigned at 6 weeks to four vaccines or 2-, 3- or 4-week schedules
Funding
not stated.
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome in one study, with low maternal antibody at 6 weeks.
What it reports
All 19 puppies vaccinated every 2 weeks and all 17 every 4 weeks seroconverted by 17 weeks, and all 20 every 3 weeks by 15 weeks. In the 2-week group 3 seroconverted only after the fourth dose at 12 weeks. Maternal antibody fell below protective levels by 6 weeks. Vaccine brands differed in response.

Claims citing this study

  • Puppies vaccinated from 6 weeks every 2, 3 or 4 weeks all seroconverted by 15 to 17 weeks MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Puppies in low-risk homes can be vaccinated every 3 to 4 weeks MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
