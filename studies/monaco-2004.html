ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Monaco 2004

Full reference
Monaco F, Bonfini B, Zaghini M, Antonucci D, Pini A, Savini G. 2004. Vaccination of cattle using monovalent modified-live vaccine against bluetongue virus serotype 2: innocuity, immunogenicity and effect on pregnancy. Veterinaria Italiana 40(4):671-675.
Design
experimental field and stabled vaccination study with later challenge
Species
cattle
Animals
21 vaccinated cows (14 pregnant) plus 2 unvaccinated controls, and 4 unvaccinated challenge controls
Population
cows at various stages of gestation, Italy
Funding
not stated. No funding statement found. Authors at the Istituto Zooprofilattico Sperimentale (G. Caporale), Teramo. Vaccine made by Onderstepoort Biological Products
Study's main limitation
Small sample of pregnant cows, in a different species and virus.
What it reports
All 14 pregnant cows completed gestation. 13 calves were healthy and one had prosencephalic hypoplasia judged to predate vaccination. No BT antibody was found in calves sampled before colostrum, BTV was not isolated from the malformed calf, and the authors concluded the BTV-2 MLV did not cross the placenta.
