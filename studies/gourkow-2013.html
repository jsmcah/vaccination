ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Gourkow 2013

Full reference
Gourkow N, Lawson JH, Hamon SC, Phillips CJC. 2013. Descriptive epidemiology of upper respiratory disease and associated risk factors in cats in an animal shelter in coastal western Canada. Canadian Veterinary Journal 54(2):132-138.
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey (pathogen PCR at intake and days 4 and 10 rather than serology, with 40-day follow-up for URD)
Species
cat
Animals
250 cats
Population
cats older than 6 months admitted to the BC SPCA Vancouver shelter, May to November 2010, housed individually
Funding
independent. Morris Animal Foundation (acknowledgments)
Study's main limitation
One shelter with intake vaccination, quarantine and single housing, so its low C. felis prevalence may not apply to crowded shelters.
What it reports
C. felis was found in 1 of 250 cats (0.4%) at intake and 3 of 221 (1.4%) by day 10. The authors concluded C. felis was not prevalent in this shelter and may not be an important risk factor for shelter cats.

Claims citing this study
