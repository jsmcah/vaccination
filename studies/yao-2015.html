ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Yao 2015

Full reference
Yao PJ, Stephenson N, Foley JE, Toussieng CR, Farver TB, Sykes JE, Fleer KA. 2015. Incidence rates and risk factors for owner-reported adverse events following vaccination of dogs that did or did not receive a Leptospira vaccine. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 247(10):1139-1145.
DOI
10.2460/javma.247.10.1139
Design
retrospective cohort (electronic records of mobile vaccine clinics)
Species
dog
Animals
130,557
Population
dogs vaccinated at VIP PetCare mobile clinics in the United States, June 2012 to March 2013
Funding
mixed. UC Davis graduate student support. Two authors held a Zoetis grant for leptospirosis diagnostics, and one author worked for VIP PetCare (author notes)
Study's main limitation
Adverse events were owner reported within 5 days, with no veterinary confirmation, and vaccine combinations were not randomized.
What it reports
Owner-reported adverse events occurred in 26.3 per 10,000 dogs overall (95% CI 23.6 to 29.2) and 53.0 per 10,000 dogs given a Leptospira vaccine (95% CI 42.8 to 64.9), adjusted OR 2.13. Hypersensitivity reactions were rare (6.5 per 10,000) and did not differ significantly with or without a Leptospira component.
