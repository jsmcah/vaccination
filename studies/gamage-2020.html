ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Gamage 2020

Full reference
Gamage BGSS, Dissanayake DRA, Silva ID. 2020. Effect of maternal antibodies on the immune response to different canine parvovirus vaccines and antibody response to selected vaccines. Sri Lanka Veterinary Journal 67(1-2):21-28.
DOI
10.4038/slvj.v67i1-2.52
Design
prospective field serology study (several vaccine groups, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
24 puppies for maternal antibody, 16 puppies in four vaccine groups, and 82 client-owned dogs given one of six monovalent vaccines
Population
puppies 6 to 8 weeks old presented to a Sri Lankan university teaching hospital
Funding
independent. University of Peradeniya research grant (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Semi-quantitative point-of-care test, four puppies per vaccine group, and convenience sampling.
What it reports
In puppies with low maternal antibody, an inactivated vaccine and a low-titer live vaccine did not produce protective antibody, while two higher-titer live vaccines did. Of six monovalent live vaccines given at 8, 11 and 14 weeks, one low-titer product failed to produce protective titers in 45% of dogs, and one puppy did not seroconvert after three doses of a high-titer vaccine.
