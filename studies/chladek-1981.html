ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Chladek 1981

Full reference
Chladek DW, Williams JM, Gerber DL, Harris LL, Murdock FM. 1981. Canine parainfluenza-Bordetella bronchiseptica vaccine immunogenicity. American Journal of Veterinary Research 42(2):266-270.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
40 dogs (3 groups of 10 vaccinated with different serials, 10 controls)
Population
dogs with low antibody to both agents (8 to 16 week old farm dogs, per Ellis 2015)
Funding
manufacturer. Authors at Philips Roxane Inc research and development (affiliation). No funding statement
Study's main limitation
Combined CPIV and Bordetella challenge, so protection cannot be credited to the CPIV fraction.
What it reports
One intranasal dose. After CPIV challenge at 18 days and Bordetella at 21 days, 0 of 30 vaccinates and 9 of 10 controls coughed. CPIV was isolated from controls for 5.1 days on average and from no vaccinate. No adverse reactions.
