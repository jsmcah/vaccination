ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Pollock 1982

Full reference
Pollock RV, Carmichael LE. 1982. Maternally derived immunity to canine parvovirus infection: transfer, decline, and interference with vaccination. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 180(1):37-42.
Design
experimental study (maternal antibody transfer, challenge and vaccination)
Species
dog
Animals
105 pups from 23 litters for antibody transfer and decline, and 76 pups from 16 litters for vaccine interference, plus small challenge groups
Population
pups of CPV-recovered, FPV-vaccinated, CPV-vaccinated and seronegative bitches in a research colony
Funding
independent. American Kennel Club, Morris Animal Foundation and Ralston Purina Co (title page footnote)
Study's main limitation
Most interference data come from MLV feline panleukopenia or inactivated vaccines, not the high-titer MLV CPV vaccines used today.
What it reports
Maternal CPV antibody declined with a half-life of 9.7 days. HI titers of 1:80 or more protected pups against challenge, while any detectable titer (1:10 or more) blocked MLV FPV and inactivated vaccines. Pups of recovered bitches often failed to respond until 14 to 16 weeks, and the latest age at response in the vaccination table is about 121 to 122 days.
