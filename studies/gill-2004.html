ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Gill 2004

Full reference
Gill M, Srinivas J, Morozov I, Smith J, Anderson C, Glover S, Champ D, Chu HJ. 2004. Three-year duration of immunity for canine distemper, adenovirus, and parvovirus after vaccination with a multivalent canine vaccine. International Journal of Applied Research in Veterinary Medicine 2(4):227-234.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
40 (32 vaccinated, 8 controls). CAV-1 challenge of 12 vaccinates and 2 controls
Population
SPF puppies 6 to 8 weeks old, seronegative, given two IM or SC doses 21 days apart
Funding
manufacturer. All authors at Fort Dodge Animal Health, maker of the vaccine tested, Duramune Adult (affiliation). No funding statement
Study's main limitation
Two controls for the CAV-1 challenge, both adults that the authors call somewhat resistant to CAV-1.
What it reports
All vaccinates had CAV-1 antibody by the second dose and stayed seropositive for 3 years. All vaccinates stayed clinically normal after CAV-1 challenge about 3 years later. IM and SC gave similar titers.
