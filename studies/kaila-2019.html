ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Kaila 2019

Full reference
Kaila M, Marjoniemi J, Nokireki T. 2019. Comparative study of rabies antibody titers of dogs vaccinated in Finland and imported street dogs vaccinated abroad. Acta Veterinaria Scandinavica 61:15.
DOI
10.1186/s13028-019-0450-8
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey
Species
dog
Animals
72 dogs (36 imported, 36 Finnish)
Population
imported street dogs from Russia and Romania and dogs vaccinated in Finland
Funding
independent. Finnish Food Safety Authority (funding statement)
Study's main limitation
Small sample, and vaccination of imported dogs could not be verified.
What it reports
19 of 36 imported dogs had titers below 0.5 IU/mL and 14 had no detectable antibody, versus 2 of 36 Finnish dogs below 0.5 IU/mL.
