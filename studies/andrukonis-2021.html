ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Andrukonis 2021

Full reference
Andrukonis A, Brown KM, Hall NJ, Protopopova A. 2021. Intake vaccinations reduced signs of canine respiratory disease during an outbreak at an animal shelter. Frontiers in Veterinary Science 8:627580.
DOI
10.3389/fvets.2021.627580
Design
retrospective observational before and after study
Species
dog
Animals
1,258 dogs (health data), 15 dogs and 6 kennels swabbed
Population
dogs over 6 weeks at a West Texas city shelter, May to August 2017
Funding
independent. PetSmart Charities funded vaccines and supplies, testing from a university startup fund (funding statement). Authors declare no conflict
Study's main limitation
Before and after design in one shelter, with an intranasal vaccine started at the same time and coughing rather than CDV infection as the outcome.
What it reports
CDV was the most common pathogen (12 of 15 dogs, 6 of 6 kennels). Once intake vaccination with Nobivac DAPPv and an intranasal vaccine began, coughing fell by 7% when at least 90% of dogs were vaccinated compared with less than 90%.
