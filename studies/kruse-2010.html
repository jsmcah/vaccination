ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Kruse 2010

Full reference
Kruse BD, Unterer S, Horlacher K, Sauter-Louis C, Hartmann K. 2010. Prognostic factors in cats with feline panleukopenia. Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine 24(6):1271-1276.
DOI
10.1111/j.1939-1676.2010.0604.x
Design
retrospective case series (prognostic factors)
Species
cat
Animals
244 cats with panleukopenia
Population
cats diagnosed at the LMU Munich small animal clinic, 1990 to 2007
Funding
independent. Performed at LMU Munich without additional funding (acknowledgment)
Study's main limitation
Retrospective records with no unaffected comparison group, and origin and vaccination data were missing for many cats.
What it reports
Survival was 51.1%. Of 184 cats with known status, 111 (60.3%) were never vaccinated, and no vaccinated cat had received a vaccine after 12 weeks of age. Vaccination status was not associated with outcome.
