ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Perley 2020

Full reference
Perley K, Burns CC, Maguire C, Shen V, Joffe E, Stefanovski D, Redding L, Germanis L, Drobatz KJ, Watson B. 2020. Retrospective evaluation of outpatient canine parvovirus treatment in a shelter-based low-cost urban clinic. Journal of Veterinary Emergency and Critical Care 30:202-208.
DOI
10.1111/vec.12941
Design
retrospective observational study (case series of treated dogs)
Species
dog
Animals
95 dogs
Population
CPV positive dogs treated as outpatients at the Pennsylvania SPCA low-cost clinic in Philadelphia, June to July 2016, when owners could not afford inpatient care
Funding
independent. Maddie's Fund (funding statement). Authors declare no conflicts
Study's main limitation
Single clinic, no inpatient comparison group, and dogs were selected as medically stable for outpatient care.
What it reports
79 of 95 (83%) survived outpatient treatment. Inpatient care was described as costing 3000 to 5000 USD, against an estimated 479 USD per dog for the outpatient protocol. Hypothermia on presentation was associated with lower survival.
