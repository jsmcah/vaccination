ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Deshpande 2009

Full reference
Deshpande MS, Jirjis FF, Tubbs AL, Jayappa H, Sweeney D, Spencer SJ, Lakshmanan N, Wasmoen TL. 2009. Evaluation of the efficacy of a canine influenza virus (H3N8) vaccine in dogs following experimental challenge. Veterinary Therapeutics 10(3):103-112.
Design
experimental challenge study (vaccinate vs placebo)
Species
dog
Animals
36 pups (21 vaccinated, 15 placebo)
Population
CIV-seronegative beagle pups 6 to 8 weeks old
Funding
manufacturer. Funded by Intervet/Schering-Plough Animal Health, which employed the authors (title page footnote, affiliations)
Study's main limitation
H3N8 vaccine and virus, manufacturer study.
What it reports
Two SQ doses 3 weeks apart, challenge 13 days after dose 2. Clinical signs (P < .001) and the magnitude and duration of shedding (P < .0001) were lower in vaccinates.
