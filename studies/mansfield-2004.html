ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Mansfield 2004

Full reference
Mansfield KL, Burr PD, Snodgrass DR, Sayers R, Fooks AR. 2004. Factors affecting the serological response of dogs and cats to rabies vaccination. Veterinary Record 154(14):423-426.
DOI
10.1136/vr.154.14.423
Design
retrospective observational (laboratory serology records)
Species
dog and cat
Animals
16,073 VLA samples plus 2,266 BioBest samples
Population
dogs and cats tested for the UK Pet Travel Scheme, 1999 to 2003
Funding
independent. DEFRA (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Pet travel population and antibody outcome only.
What it reports
4.12% of dogs and 2.85% of cats failed to reach 0.5 IU/mL. Vaccine, interval to sampling, age and country affected failure. Two doses raised mean titers in dogs but not in cats.

Claims citing this study

  • More than 97% of cats reach adequate antibody after one rabies dose Rabies (killed or recombinant)
  • A minority of dogs fail to reach 0.5 IU/mL after a first dose, and most respond to a booster Rabies (killed or recombinant)
