ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Baker 1959

Full reference
Baker JA, Robson DS, Gillespie JH, Burgher JA, Doughty MF. 1959. A nomograph that predicts the age to vaccinate puppies against distemper. The Cornell Veterinarian 49(1):158-167.
Design
controlled serology study (nomograph with field test)
Species
dog
Animals
68 puppies from 15 dams in the first field test
Population
puppies of dams with known titers, Cornell
Funding
independent. Supported in part by the American Kennel Club and an NIH grant (footnote)
Study's main limitation
1950s methods, and the nomograph reached only 82% adequate response before it was adjusted.
What it reports
Maternal antibody declined about 44% per week and some puppies could not be immunized until 12 weeks. The nomograph predicts vaccination age from the dam's titer, and one week was added after the first field test.

Claims citing this study

  • Some puppies cannot be immunized against CDV until about 12 weeks because of maternal antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
