What it reports
Six sequence types in serogroups Icterohaemorrhagiae, Australis, Sejroe and Pomona. Some regularly vaccinated dogs were infected with serogroups not in their vaccine, and three with Icterohaemorrhagiae, which is in all vaccines

Claims citing this study

  • Vaccination reduces disease severity and shedding but does not prevent infection or shedding Leptospirosis (quadrivalent)
