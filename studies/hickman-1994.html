Hickman 1994

Full reference
Hickman MA, Reubel GH, Hoffman DE, Morris JG, Rogers QR, Pedersen NC. 1994. An epizootic of feline herpesvirus, type 1 in a large specific pathogen-free cat colony and attempts to eradicate the infection by identification and culling of carriers. Laboratory Animals 28(4):320-329.

Design

Species
cat
Animals
690 cats in two colonies, 12 recovered pregnant queens tested through lactation
Population
SPF research and breeding colonies, UC Davis
Funding
not stated in the extracted text
Study's main limitation
Research colony rather than shelter, and only 12 queens were followed through lactation.
What it reports
Four percent of recovered cats were actively shedding before corticosteroid treatment and 21% shed after one injection. None of 12 recovered pregnant queens shed during gestation, but 2 shed between 1 and 3 weeks of lactation. Disease was more severe in older cats, lactating queens and kittens 3 to 6 weeks old.
