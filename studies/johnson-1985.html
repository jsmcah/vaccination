Johnson 1985

Full reference
Johnson RP, Povey RC. 1985. Vaccination against feline viral rhinotracheitis in kittens with maternally derived feline viral rhinotracheitis antibodies. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 186(2):149-152.
Design
experimental challenge study (IN MLV vs killed parenteral)
Species
cat
Population
kittens with and without maternal antibody, IN dose at 5 weeks or parenteral doses at 5 and 7 weeks
Study's main limitation
No PDF, so methods, group sizes and funding could not be checked.
What it reports
Per the Zotero abstract, seroconversion was delayed 5 to 10 days by maternal antibody, both vaccines gave satisfactory protection against disease but not infection, and both can be used in kittens with maternal antibody.
