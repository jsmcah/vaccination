ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Peda 2023

Full reference
Peda A, Samaniego P, Daugherty C, Wood T, Wang C, Knobel D. 2023. Does perioperative administration of rabies vaccine in dogs undergoing surgical sterilization induce an adequate antibody response? Vaccines 11(9):1418.
DOI
10.3390/vaccines11091418
Design
prospective field serology study (historical control)
Species
dog
Animals
50 perioperative dogs and 57 historical controls
Population
never-vaccinated dogs 5 to 96 months old at spay/neuter events in St Kitts and Nevis
Funding
independent. Ross University intramural grant
Study's main limitation
Historical controls differed in age and sampling time.
What it reports
84% of perioperative dogs and 96% of controls reached 0.5 IU/mL at 28 to 35 days, with no significant difference after adjustment for age and sex.
