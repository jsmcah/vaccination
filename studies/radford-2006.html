What it reports
States that existing FCV vaccines are generally safe and control most clinical disease but do not induce sterilizing immunity, so vaccinated cats can be infected and shed field virus. Notes occasional vaccine breakdowns, VSD outbreaks mostly in vaccinated cats, and occasional vaccine-induced disease with live vaccines. Most vaccines are based on single strains such as F9 or 255, and a two-strain vaccine (431 and G1) had been marketed in Europe. Calls for more broadly cross-reactive vaccines, regular monitoring against contemporary field panels, and vaccines that reduce shedding.
