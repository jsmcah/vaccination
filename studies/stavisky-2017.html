ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Stavisky 2017

Full reference
Stavisky J, Dean RS, Molloy MH. 2017. Prevalence of and risk factors for FIV and FeLV infection in two shelters in the United Kingdom (2011-2012). Veterinary Record 181(17):451.
DOI
10.1136/vr.103857
Design
cross-sectional prevalence study
Species
cat
Animals
608 cats tested for FeLV (473 and 135)
Population
cats entering two RSPCA rehoming centres in the UK, kittens under 3 months excluded
Study's main limitation
Two UK shelters, point-of-care testing only (from abstract).
What it reports
FeLV prevalence was 3% (14 of 473) in one shelter and 0% (0 of 135) in the other. FeLV-positive cats were older than uninfected cats (median 4.75 versus 3.5 years), and thin body condition and musculoskeletal disease were associated with FeLV.
