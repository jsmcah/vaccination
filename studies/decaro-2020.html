Study's main limitation
Narrative review, and the statements on safety in pregnancy and in 4 week old pups rest on the first author's personal observation.
What it reports
States that some recent MLV CPV vaccines have been shown innocuous for fetuses and 4 week old pups and are registered for use in pregnancy and young pups (personal observation). Notes WSAVA advises against MLV CPV vaccine in pups under 4 to 6 weeks and in pregnancy. Reports that all marketed CPV vaccines are registered for parenteral use only, that oral and intranasal use is off label, and that WSAVA judged these routes less effective than parenteral. Gives the window of susceptibility as usually 2 to 3 weeks.
