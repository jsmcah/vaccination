Design
cross-sectional PCR survey
Species
dog
Animals

Population
asymptomatic dogs at US shelters in four regions
Funding
manufacturer. Both authors employed by Zoetis (affiliation, conflict statement)
Study's main limitation
PCR positivity without viral load for most positives, and 10 CDV positives came from one shelter.
What it reports
37 of 503 (7.4%) asymptomatic dogs were PCR positive for CDV, mostly in the West. Only one had a viral load indicating active infection, and that dog developed clinical distemper 2 weeks later.

Claims citing this study
