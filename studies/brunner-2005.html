ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Brunner 2005

Full reference
Brunner C, Kanellos T, Meli ML, Sutton DJ, Gisler R, Gomes-Keller MA, Hofmann-Lehmann R, Lutz H. 2006. Antibody induction after combined application of an adjuvanted recombinant FeLV vaccine and a multivalent modified live virus vaccine with a chlamydial component. Vaccine 24(11):1838-1846.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2005.10.030
Design
controlled serology study (three randomized arms, MLV FCV/FHV-1/FPV/C. felis with or without recombinant FeLV in one syringe vs FeLV alone, no challenge)
Species
cat
Animals
30 SPF kittens (10 per group)
Population
SPF kittens 7 to 8 weeks old at arrival, vaccinated SC at 8 to 9 weeks (day 0) and again at day 21, bled weekly to day 48
Funding
manufacturer. Two authors (Kanellos, Sutton) were Intervet UK employees, the vaccines were Intervet Nobivac Forcat and Nobivac FeLV, and the study was intended in part for a regulatory submission. One author had a Swiss National Science Foundation professorship (affiliations, introduction, acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Ten SPF kittens per group, IFA antibody only with no challenge and no protective threshold, and median titres only.
What it reports
FCV IFA antibody titres were significantly higher on days 20 to 48 in kittens given MLV plus FeLV in one syringe than in kittens given MLV alone (Mann-Whitney, P < 0.05). Results are shown as median titres by group in Figure 1, with no individual titres or protection rates. Combined use was safe apart from local swelling attributed to the FeLV adjuvant.
