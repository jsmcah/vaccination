Roccabianca 2016

Full reference
Roccabianca P, Avallone G, Rodriguez A, Crippa L, Lepri E, Giudice C, Caniatti M, Moore PF, Affolter VK. 2016. Cutaneous lymphoma at injection sites. Pathological, immunophenotypical, and molecular characterization in 17 cats. Veterinary Pathology 53(4):823-832.
DOI
10.1177/0300985815623620
Design
case series
Species
cat
Animals
17 cats (from 47 primary cutaneous lymphomas)
Population
cats in Italy with cutaneous lymphoma at a prior injection site, mean age 11.3 years
Funding
not stated. No funding statement in the accepted manuscript
Study's main limitation
Retrospective pathology series with no denominator or comparison group, and only 5 cats had received FeLV vaccine.
What it reports
All 17 cats had FVRCP and 5 had FeLV vaccine. Tumors arose 15 days to about 9 years after injection where known. FeLV gp70 or p27 was expressed in 10 of 17 tumors, including 5 of 8 FeLV-seronegative cats. The authors suggest injection inflammation and FeLV reactivation may have contributed.

Claims citing this study

  • Injection-site lymphomas expressing FeLV proteins have been reported, a reason to test before vaccinating FeLV
