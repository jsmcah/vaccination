Pardo 2007

Full reference
Pardo MC, Tanner P, Bauman J, Silver K, Fischer L. 2007. Immunization of puppies in the presence of maternally derived antibodies against canine distemper virus. Journal of Comparative Pathology 137:S72-S75.
DOI
10.1016/j.jcpa.2007.04.015
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
15 puppies (12 vaccinated, 3 controls)
Population
puppies 7 to 9 weeks old with maternal antibody to CDV
Funding
manufacturer. All authors at Merial (affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Small, manufacturer study.
What it reports
Twelve pups with maternal antibody given canarypox-vectored CDV vaccine were protected against IV challenge. All three unvaccinated littermates became ill and two died.

Claims citing this study

  • Recombinant CDV vaccine protected puppies against challenge, including puppies with maternal antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Two vaccine doses protected puppies with maternal antibody against CDV challenge MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
