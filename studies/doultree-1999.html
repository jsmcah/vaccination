Study's main limitation
Single laboratory vaccine strain at high titers, framed for human norovirus control, with no cats, shelters or transmission studied.
What it reports
Complete inactivation needed 1000 ppm freshly reconstituted granular hypochlorite or 5000 ppm pre-made hypochlorite solution. Glutaraldehyde and iodine also inactivated FCV, but a quaternary ammonium product (at double the recommended concentration), a detergent and 75% ethanol did not. Dried FCV survived 21 to 28 days at room temperature, but less than 1 day at 37°C.
