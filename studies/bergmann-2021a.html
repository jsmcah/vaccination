ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Bergmann 2021a

Full reference
Bergmann M, Holzheu M, Zablotski Y, Speck S, Truyen U, Hartmann K. 2021. Evaluation of a point-of-care test for pre-vaccination testing to detect antibodies against canine adenoviruses in dogs. Viruses 13(2):183.
DOI
10.3390/v13020183
Design
diagnostic accuracy study
Species
dog
Animals
238 (198 owned, 40 SPF)
Population
privately owned and SPF dogs in Germany, with virus neutralization as reference
Funding
mixed. Biogal supplied the test and had no role in the study. Hartmann and Bergmann have received funding from or used products of several vaccine makers (funding and conflict statements)
Study's main limitation
Single test brand, and specificity estimates in owned dogs rest on few antibody-negative dogs.
What it reports
Specificity for CAV-1 antibody was 56% in owned dogs and 88% overall. For CAV-2 it was 65% and 90%. 92% of owned dogs had CAV-1 neutralizing antibody.
