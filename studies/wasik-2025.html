What it reports
North American H3N2 shows local outbreaks, regional die-outs and repeated reintroductions from Asia. Observed outbreaks died out after 1 to about 6 months. No outbreaks were reported from late 2019 through 2020. A 2022 introduction caused outbreaks continuing into 2024. H3N8 died out in North American dogs in 2017. Vaccination in the face of an outbreak is unlikely to change spread.
