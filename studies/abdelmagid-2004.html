ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Abdelmagid 2004

Full reference
Abdelmagid OY, Larson L, Payne L, Tubbs A, Wasmoen T, Schultz R. 2004. Evaluation of the efficacy and duration of immunity of a canine combination vaccine against virulent parvovirus, infectious canine hepatitis virus, and distemper virus experimental challenges. Veterinary Therapeutics 5(3).
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
10 vaccinated dogs, 7 adult controls, 3 sets of 5 young control puppies
Population
seronegative puppies vaccinated at 7 to 8 weeks with two doses 3 weeks apart
Funding
manufacturer. Funded by Schering-Plough Animal Health, which markets the vaccine, and four of six authors worked there (funding note, affiliations)
Study's main limitation
Ten vaccinated dogs and a two-dose series, so it does not inform single-dose protection.
What it reports
Nine of 10 vaccinates had high CDV titers after the second dose and one never developed neutralizing antibody. At CDV challenge 56.5 months after vaccination all vaccinates survived and 90% had no clinical disease.
