What it reports
FeLV antigen prevalence was 3.1% overall, 2.6% in shelters and 3.4% in clinics. Outdoor access (OR 2.0, 95% CI 1.8 to 2.2), intact male sex, adult age and illness were associated with positivity. 5.5% of cats with abscesses or bite wounds were positive.

Claims citing this study

  • FeLV antigen positivity is more common in cats with bite wounds or abscesses, outdoor access, or intact males, which fits spread through close contact and fighting FeLV
  • FeLV antigen prevalence among cats tested in the USA and Canada was about 3%, and lower in shelters than in clinics FeLV
  • AAFP 2020 advises testing cats before FeLV vaccination, and the ASV position statement asks shelters to weigh the cost of testing low-risk, individually housed cats. With shelter prevalence near 3%, blanket intake screening finds few positive cats FeLV
  • Some clinic cats have outdoor access or multi-cat households that put them at meaningful risk FeLV
