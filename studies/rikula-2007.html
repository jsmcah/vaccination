Study's main limitation
Herd immunity estimated from vaccine sales and vaccine take rates, not measured.
What it reports
Herd immunity was only 50% to 65% in 1990 to 1993 because low-take vaccines dominated. Replacing them raised it to 90%, coinciding with the end of the 1994 to 1995 epidemic. About 70% then controlled the disease despite imports.
