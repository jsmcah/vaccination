ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Shade 1979

Full reference
Shade FJ, Goodnow RA. 1979. Intranasal immunization of dogs against Bordetella bronchiseptica-induced tracheobronchitis (kennel cough) with modified live-Bordetella bronchiseptica vaccine. American Journal of Veterinary Research 40(9):1241-1243.
Design
experimental challenge study (two experiments, unvaccinated controls, allocation method not stated)
Species
dog
Animals
41 dogs in two experiments (experiment 1, 20 vaccinated and 11 controls. Experiment 2, 5 vaccinated and 5 controls)
Population
mixed-breed dogs 6 to 8 weeks old, culture negative for Bb (experiment 1). Age in experiment 2 not stated
Funding
manufacturer. Authors are at Burns-Biotec Laboratories Inc, Omaha, which prepared the S-55 vaccine in house (author affiliation footnote and acknowledgements). No funding statement
Study's main limitation
Small groups with allocation and observer masking not described, a single strain challenge 14 days after vaccination, and a scanned copy that is partly illegible in the summary.
What it reports
After aerosol challenge 14 days after one IN dose of live S-55 Bb, 1 of 20 vaccinated and 11 of 11 control dogs coughed (95% reduction), with a mean of 0.15 versus 9.73 days of coughing (P < 0.001). Vaccinated dogs had lower nasal counts of challenge organisms (5.52 x 10^3 versus 2.56 x 10^6 per mL at day 28, P < 0.05). In experiment 2, 0 of 5 vaccinated and 5 of 5 control dogs had pulmonary lesions and excess tracheal mucus.
