ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Mila 2014

Full reference
Mila H, Grellet A, Desario C, Feugier A, Decaro N, Buonavoglia C, Chastant-Maillard S. 2014. Protection against canine parvovirus type 2 infection in puppies by colostrum-derived antibodies. Journal of Nutritional Science 3:e54.
DOI
10.1017/jns.2014.57
Design
prospective observational cohort (serology and natural infection, unvaccinated puppies)
Species
dog
Animals
79 puppies from 26 litters
Population
unvaccinated puppies in one French commercial breeding kennel with natural CPV circulation, followed from birth to 56 days
Funding
not stated. Partially funded by Royal Canin SAS, a pet food company, and two authors were Royal Canin employees (acknowledgements, affiliations)
Study's main limitation
One breeding kennel, and the puppies were not vaccinated, so it describes maternal antibody decline and natural infection rather than vaccine response.
What it reports
At 2 days old, maternal HI titers ranged from 1:10 to 1:1280 and 43% of puppies had 1:160 or less. Maternal antibody half-life was 13.4 days. At 56 days none of the 67 surviving puppies had a titer of 1:80 or more, 63 of 67 seroconverted from field infection, and 67 of 70 shed CPV.
