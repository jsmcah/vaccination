Castillo-Aliaga 2026

Full reference
Castillo-Aliaga C, Dunham S, McCallum-Chanter H, Tarlinton R. 2026. Twenty-year lifetime histories of FIV- and FeLV-infected cats in a sanctuary in the UK indicate an effect of retroviral infection on longevity. Veterinary Record e1-e8.
DOI
10.1002/vetr.70806
Design
retrospective cohort study
Species
cat
Animals
132
Population
Cats in one UK sanctuary, 22 uninfected, 82 FIV+, 24 FeLV+ and 4 FeLV/FIV+
Funding
Agencia Nacional de Investigacion y Desarrollo, Chile (scholarship)
Study's main limitation
Small groups. FeLV status mostly by point-of-care antigen test, without separating progressive and regressive infection
What it reports
Median life expectancy was 160.3 months for uninfected and 41.5 months for FeLV+ cats (p < 0.001). Neoplasia, mostly lymphoma, was associated with FeLV (13 of 24 FeLV+ cats). Anemia occurred in 4 of 24 FeLV+ and 0 of 22 uninfected cats.
