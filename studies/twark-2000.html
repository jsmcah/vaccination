ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Twark 2000

Full reference
Twark L, Dodds WJ. 2000. Clinical use of serum parvovirus and distemper virus antibody titers for determining revaccination strategies in healthy dogs. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 217(7):1021-1024.
DOI
10.2460/javma.2000.217.1021
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey (laboratory submissions, described by the authors as a case series)
Species
dog
Animals
1441 dogs
Population
dogs 6 weeks to 17 years old whose serum was submitted to a commercial diagnostic laboratory from 17 US states and 6 Canadian provinces
Funding
not stated. No funding statement. Authors from Hemopet and Antech Diagnostics, a laboratory offering titer testing (affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Laboratory submissions with an IFA cutoff calibrated on 61 paired samples, and vaccination history known for only about a third of dogs.
What it reports
1370 of 1441 (95.1%) had adequate and 71 (4.9%) inadequate CPV antibody. Of the 71, vaccination history was available for 19, last vaccinated under 1 year earlier in 2, 1 to 2 years in 13 and 2 to 7 years in 4.

Claims citing this study

  • Most adult dogs already have high CPV titers, and few show a rise after a booster MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
