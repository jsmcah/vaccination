Bergmann 2020 (dogs)

Full reference
Bergmann M, Freisl M, Zablotski Y, Speck S, Truyen U, Hartmann K. 2020. Antibody response to canine adenovirus-2 virus vaccination in healthy adult dogs. Viruses 12(10):1198.

Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome only, in owned dogs with vaccination histories rather than shelter intake.
What it reports
87% (90 of 97, 95% CI 85.61 to 96.70) had CAV antibody before revaccination and 6% (6 of 97, 95% CI 2.60 to 13.11) had a 4-fold rise. Four dogs (4.1%) had no antibody before or after. Mild adverse events in 36.1% (35 of 97). More than 5 years since vaccination was associated with lack of antibody (OR 25.0, p = 0.006).
