What it reports
All isolates were L. interrogans serovar Canicola but genomically distinct, suggesting multiple sources. Peak cases had higher odds of indoor congregate facility exposure. None of 47 dogs with known history was completely vaccinated. 29 of 40 (73%) had renal impairment only and 6 of 40 (15%) renal and liver impairment. 54 of 59 (92%) survived to discharge, some still azotemic. Urine PCR was positive in 49 of 54 (91%)
