ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Cavalli 2020

Full reference
Cavalli A, Desario C, Marinaro M, Losurdo M, Camero M, Decaro N, Catella C, Lanave G, Buonavoglia C. 2020. Oral administration of modified live canine parvovirus type 2b induces systemic immune response. Vaccine 38(2):115-118.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2019.10.016
Design
controlled serology study (single arm, oral route, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
14 pups (one litter of 11 plus 3 pups from other owners)
Population
privately owned healthy 40 day old pups with MDA HI titers of 1:20 to 1:160, Italy
Funding
not stated. No funding statement. Authors declare no competing interests. Commercial monovalent vaccine (Canigen Puppy 2b, Virbac) with no statement that it was donated
Study's main limitation
Fourteen pups, 11 of them littermates, with no parenteral comparison group, no challenge, and no sampling before day 15.
What it reports
A commercial monovalent MLV CPV-2b vaccine given in a biscuit bait seroconverted 13 of 14 pups by day 15 (HI 1:1280 to 1:10,240), including 1 of 2 pups with MDA of 1:160. The pup that spat the bait out twice seroconverted after a second oral dose. Vaccine virus was shed in feces from day 4 to day 15 or longer. No local or general reactions were seen.

Claims citing this study

  • Oral monovalent MLV CPV-2b vaccine seroconverted pups with moderate maternal antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
