ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Larson 2006

Full reference
Larson LJ, Schultz RD. 2006. Effect of vaccination with recombinant canine distemper virus vaccine immediately before exposure under shelter-like conditions. Veterinary Therapeutics 7(2):113-118.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
16 puppies (12 in IV challenge part, 4 in contact part)
Population
CDV-seronegative puppies 10 to 12 weeks old (part 1) and 12 weeks old (part 2)
Funding
mixed. Study funded by gifts to the University of Wisconsin vaccine program, and publication sponsored by Merial, maker of the recombinant vaccine (footnote)
Study's main limitation
Two dogs per vaccine per time point, unclear clinical scoring, no virology, and no controls in the contact part.
What it reports
MLV or recombinant CDV given 1 week before IV challenge prevented clinical signs. Given 15 minutes to 4 hours before challenge, both groups became sick and recovered, while all 4 unvaccinated controls died. Four recombinant-vaccinated pups placed with infected dogs 4 hours after vaccination stayed healthy.

Claims citing this study

  • MLV or recombinant CDV given one week before IV challenge prevented disease, but not when given hours before MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Recombinant CDV vaccine protected puppies against challenge, including puppies with maternal antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
