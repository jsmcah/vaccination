Spindel 2018

Full reference
Spindel ME, Krecic MR, Slater MR, Vigil N. 2018. Evaluation of a community's risk for canine parvovirus and distemper using antibody testing and GIS mapping of animal shelter intakes. Journal of Applied Animal Welfare Science 21(4):362-374.
DOI
10.1080/10888705.2018.1435281
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey
Species
dog
Animals
1,056 dogs tested of 2,745 admitted

Funding
manufacturer. Funded by Zoetis and the ASPCA, with one Zoetis author and Zoetis test kits (funding statement, acknowledgments)
Study's main limitation

What it reports
464 of 1,056 (44%) were CDV antibody positive. Antibody-negative dogs clustered in defined community areas.
