ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Ek-Kommonen 1997

Full reference
Ek-Kommonen C, Sihvonen L, Pekkanen K, Rikula U, Nuotio L. 1997. Outbreak of canine distemper in vaccinated dogs in Finland. Veterinary Record 141(15):380-383.
DOI
10.1136/vr.141.15.380
Design
outbreak investigation (national laboratory surveillance with vaccine history and serosurvey)
Species
dog
Animals
865 laboratory-confirmed cases among an estimated 5000 or more, and 4676 serum samples from healthy vaccinated dogs
Population
dogs in Finland during the 1994 to 1995 epidemic, 73% of confirmed cases aged 3 to 24 months
Funding
government. Authors at the National Veterinary and Food Research Institute and the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry (affiliations)
Study's main limitation
Mortality is an estimate from clinician records rather than a followed cohort, and clinical signs are as reported by submitting veterinarians.
What it reports
Clinical signs ranged from conjunctivitis, fever and anorexia to respiratory and gastrointestinal illness, with an estimated mortality of 30%. Of the confirmed cases, 487 had been vaccinated at least once and 351 had a complete vaccination history, and one vaccine was overrepresented relative to its market share.
