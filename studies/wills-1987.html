ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Wills 1987

Full reference
Wills JM, Gruffydd-Jones TJ, Richmond SJ, Gaskell RM, Bourne FJ. 1987. Effect of vaccination on feline Chlamydia psittaci infection. Infection and Immunity 55(11):2653-2657.
DOI
10.1128/iai.55.11.2653-2657.1987
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
cat
Animals
10 SPF cats (5 vaccinated, 5 unvaccinated)
Population
SPF cats given one SQ dose of a live commercial vaccine (Psittacoid, Fromm Laboratories) and challenged in the eye 4 weeks later with a British feline pneumonitis isolate
Funding
not stated. A grant from Duphar Veterinary Ltd, a commercial company, funded the work (acknowledgments). The paper does not say whether Duphar made vaccines. The vaccine tested was a Fromm Laboratories product
Study's main limitation
Five cats per group, one dose of a live vaccine, and a single challenge at 4 weeks.
What it reports
Vaccination reduced the severity of conjunctivitis but did not reduce the number of organisms shed from the eye or prevent spread to the gut and genital tract. Conjunctival shedding lasted longer in vaccinated cats (63 to 68 days) than in controls (29 to 46 days). Its introduction notes that earlier vaccine studies gave conflicting results.
