ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Ellis 2017

Full reference
Ellis JA, Gow SP, Lee LB, Lacoste S, Ball EC. 2017. Comparative efficacy of intranasal and injectable vaccines in stimulating Bordetella bronchiseptica-reactive anamnestic antibody responses in household dogs. Canadian Veterinary Journal 58:809-815.
Design
controlled serology study (randomized, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
144 adult household dogs
Population
adult household dogs given intranasal Bordetella vaccine about 12 months earlier
Funding
mixed. Supported in part by an unrestricted Zoetis grant, and one author is a Zoetis employee (acknowledgments, conflict of interest statement)
Study's main limitation
Bordetella antibody outcome only, so it says nothing about CPIV.
What it reports
Intranasal and injectable single-component Bordetella vaccines produced similar changes in serum IgG and nasal IgA, and intranasal vaccine produced higher serum IgA.
