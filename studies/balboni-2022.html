What it reports
30 of 59 dogs (50.9%) had MAT or molecular evidence of infection, and 22 (37.3%) were seropositive to serogroup Sejroe. MLST identified L. borgpetersenii serogroup Sejroe (ST155), which no vaccine used there contains. 30 dogs were regularly vaccinated (24 tetravalent, 6 bivalent), 9 of them within 15 weeks of sampling. The other 29 had a bivalent vaccine more than a year earlier.
