ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Maboni 2019

Full reference
Maboni G, Seguel M, Lorton A, Berghaus R, Sanchez S. 2019. Canine infectious respiratory disease: new insights into the etiology and epidemiology of associated pathogens. PLOS ONE 14(4):e0215817.
DOI
10.1371/journal.pone.0215817
Design
retrospective observational study of diagnostic submissions
Species
dog
Animals
559
Population
client-owned dogs with respiratory PCR panels at a Georgia diagnostic laboratory, 2011 to 2017
Funding
mixed. Athens Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory, with the first author supported by the University of Georgia and Boehringer Ingelheim (funding statement)
Study's main limitation
Diagnostic submissions, mostly from the southeastern US, with no shelter sampling and patchy vaccination data.
What it reports
CAV was detected in 2.5% (14 of 559) of samples. The authors suggest low detection of CAV, CDV and Bordetella reflects vaccination. Coinfection and young age were associated with severity.
