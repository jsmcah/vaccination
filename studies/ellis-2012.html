What it reports
Experimental CPIV alone causes absent or mild signs from 7 to 9 days after exposure. CPIV spread rapidly in crowded kennels before vaccination. Both parenteral and intranasal CPIV vaccines reduce upper respiratory disease, but true duration of clinical immunity is unknown and likely well under 3 years.
