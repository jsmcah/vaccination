ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Pedersen 2004

Full reference
Pedersen NC, Sato R, Foley JE, Poland AM. 2004. Common virus infections in cats, before and after being placed in shelters, with emphasis on feline enteric coronavirus. Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery 6(2):83-88.
DOI
10.1016/j.jfms.2003.08.008
Design
prospective observational shedding study
Species
cat
Animals
162 cats at entry, 60 resampled at 1 week
Population
healthy cats relinquished to two northern California shelters
Funding
independent. Center for Companion Animal Health and Maddie's Shelter Medicine Program, UC Davis (acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Two shelters, small resampled group, and virus isolation cannot separate reactivation from new infection.
What it reports
FHV shedding was 4% (7 of 162) at entry, mainly in juveniles, and 52% (31 of 60) one week later. Authors attribute the rise to both reactivation and new infection.
