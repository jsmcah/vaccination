Species

Study's main limitation

What it reports
26 of 198 dogs (13.1%, 95% CI 8.4% to 17.8%) PCR positive in urine and 38 of 211 (18.0%) MAT positive. 23 of the 26 PCR-positive dogs came from one shelter (KR). All cats negative. Shed species was L. interrogans
