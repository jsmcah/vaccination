ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Spiri 2021

Full reference
Spiri AM, Novacco M, Meli ML, Stirn M, Riond B, Fogle JE, Boretti FS, Herbert I, Hosie MJ, Hofmann-Lehmann R. 2021. Modified-live feline calicivirus vaccination elicits cellular immunity against a current feline calicivirus field strain in an experimental feline challenge study. Viruses 13(9):1736.

cat
Animals

Population
SPF cats vaccinated at 15 and 18 weeks of age in Switzerland

Study's main limitation
Five cats per group, many outcomes and time points tested without correction, and the vaccinated cats' second challenge came after both groups had already been infected once.
What it reports
Cats got a MLV trivalent vaccine containing FCV F9 SQ twice 21 days apart, were challenged with field strain FCV 273 7 months later, revaccinated 11 months after that, and challenged with FCV 27 1 month after revaccination. IFA seroconversion in vaccinates occurred between days 7 and 13 after the first injection (titers 80 to 320 at day 13), peaking at up to 1280 between days 27 and 40. Neutralizing antibody to F9 appeared by day 13, but no vaccinate serum neutralized FCV 273, FCV 27 or a Swiss VS-FCV strain. Vaccinates developed a Th1 cytokine response and had IFN-gamma-releasing PBMCs against F9 and FCV 273 before challenge. After challenge I they had higher CD5, CD8 and CD4/CD25 counts and higher perforin and granzyme B transcription than controls. The first challenge induced cross-neutralizing antibody to FCV 27 in all cats, and challenge II caused no clinical signs in any cat.
