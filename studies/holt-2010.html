ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Holt 2010

Full reference
Holt DE, Moyer MR, Brown DC. 2010. Serologic prevalence of antibodies against canine influenza virus (H3N8) in dogs in a metropolitan animal shelter. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 237(1):71-73.
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey (random selection of shelter dogs)
Species
dog
Animals
74 dogs
Population
randomly selected dogs in one City of Philadelphia animal shelter, October to December 2008, all given IN Bb plus CPiV vaccine at admission
Funding
independent. Grant from the University of Pennsylvania School of Veterinary Medicine Center for Infectious Diseases (title page footnote)
Study's main limitation
Single shelter, single sample per dog and only 74 dogs, and the outcome is CIV H3N8 seropositivity, not respiratory disease.
What it reports
31 of 74 dogs (42%) were CIV seropositive, 6 of 39 (15%) housed 7 days or less and 25 of 35 (71%) housed 8 days or more. Each 3 days in the shelter increased the odds of seropositivity (OR 2.2, 95% CI 1.5 to 3.4). Coughing (19% versus 21%) and nasal discharge were not associated with seropositivity.
