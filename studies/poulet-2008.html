ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Poulet 2008

Full reference
Poulet H, Jas D, Lemeter C, Coupier C, Brunet S. 2008. Efficacy of a bivalent inactivated non-adjuvanted feline calicivirus vaccine: relation between in vitro cross-neutralization and heterologous protection in vivo. Vaccine 26(29):3647-3654.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2008.04.082
Design
experimental challenge study (11 vaccinate vs unvaccinated control studies, heterologous FCV challenge including 3 VS-FCV strains, plus in vitro cross-neutralization panel)
Species
cat
Animals
82 vaccinated kittens across 11 studies (2 to 12 per study) with an equal number of unvaccinated controls, plus F9 MLV groups in studies 1 and 2 and an adjuvanted FCV255 group in study 2 (sizes of these groups not given)
Population
SPF kittens about 8 to 9 weeks old from Charles River laboratories, France, seronegative for FCV
Funding
manufacturer. All authors at Merial S.A.S., Lyon, maker of the vaccine tested (PUREVAX RCPCh-FeLV components), and studies approved by the Merial Ethical Committee (author affiliation and methods). No funding statement
Study's main limitation
Small groups (2 to 12 vaccinates) with clinical score reduction as the main outcome and only symbols (ns, t) for statistics. The cross-neutralization data used sera from experimentally infected cats, not vaccinated cats. All work was done by the vaccine maker on its own product.
What it reports
Cats got the inactivated, non-adjuvanted bivalent vaccine (FCV431 plus FCVG1) twice SQ 4 weeks apart and were challenged oronasally 1 to 4 weeks after vaccination. Clinical score reduction ranged from 0% to 80%. It was more than 67% for challenge strains neutralized by both vaccine-strain antisera, 57% (FCV220) and 18% (FCV393, ns) when only one antiserum neutralized, and 0% (ns) against VS-FCV ENVT, which neither antiserum neutralized. Against VS-FCV 33585, mortality was 58% (7/12) in controls and 18% (2/11) in vaccinates. In studies 1 and 2 (FCV255 challenge), F9 MLV reduced scores by 54% and 49% versus 73% to 75% for the bivalent vaccine, and adjuvanted FCV255 vaccine reduced scores by 86% against homologous challenge. Excretion was reduced 46% to 88%. In vitro, FCVG1 and FCV431 antisera neutralized 81% (48/59) and 85% (50/59) of isolates, ranging 60% (Japan) to 100% (US) by origin, versus 25% (15/59) and 24% (14/59) for F9 and FCV255 antisera. In study 3, vaccinates had no detectable neutralizing antibody to FCV220 before challenge yet had reduced clinical signs.
