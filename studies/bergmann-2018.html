ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Bergmann 2018

Full reference
Bergmann M, Schwertler S, Reese S, Speck S, Truyen U, Hartmann K. 2018. Antibody response to feline panleukopenia virus vaccination in healthy adult cats. Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery 20(12):1087-1093.
DOI
10.1177/1098612X17747740
Design
prospective field serology study (single vaccination, no challenge, no unvaccinated group)
Species
cat
Animals
112 healthy adult cats
Population
healthy adult client-owned cats in Germany
Funding
manufacturer. Merial, Lyon, France (funding statement, conflict of interest statement and acknowledgements)
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome only (4-fold HI rise by day 28), no challenge, and wide confidence intervals for the main associations.
What it reports
64.3% (72/112) had HI titers of 1:40 or more before vaccination and only 47.3% (53/112) had an adequate (4-fold) response. Lack of pre-vaccination antibody was associated with adequate response (OR 23.10, 95% CI 5.4 to 98.8), and no cat with a pre-vaccination titer of 1:160 or more had a 4-fold rise.
