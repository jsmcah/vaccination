Beall 2025

Full reference
Beall MJ, Moore D, Turner S, Levy JK. 2025. Feline leukemia virus lifetime study. Whole blood samples increase detection of low positive cats with extended long-term survival. Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery.
DOI
10.1177/1098612X251379219
Design
prospective cohort study
Species
cat
Animals
127
Population
FeLV p27 antigen-positive cats in the USA, mostly adopted, followed up to 8 years
Funding
Maddie's Fund and EveryCat Health Foundation, with some testing supported by IDEXX. Two authors are IDEXX employees
Study's main limitation
Survival after enrollment was owner reported. Classification as high or low positive is a proxy for progressive or regressive infection
What it reports
High positive cats had a median survival of 1.4 years and 2 of 90 were still alive at 7 to 8 years. Low positive cats had not reached median survival, with 19 of 29 alive. 26 of 90 high positive cats were alive at 3 years and 13 of 90 at 5 years or more. Whole blood on SNAP found more low positive cats than plasma or serum.
