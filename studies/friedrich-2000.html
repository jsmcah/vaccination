ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Friedrich 2000

Full reference
Friedrich K, Truyen U. 2000. Untersuchung der Wirksamkeit von Parvovirusimpfstoffen und der Effektivität zweier Impfschemata (Efficacy of parvovirus vaccines and effectiveness of two vaccination protocols). Der Praktische Tierarzt 81(12):988-994.
Design
prospective field serology study (two vaccination protocols, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
388 puppies from 58 litters (181 protocol 1, 207 protocol 2)
Population
privately bred puppies of many breeds in Germany, vaccinated by breeders with products of four manufacturers
Funding
not stated. No funding statement found. Vaccines of Intervet, Merial, Pfizer and Virbac were used
Study's main limitation
Non-random allocation for litters with past parvovirus problems, antibody outcome only, and the paper is in German.
What it reports
After a monovalent MLV CPV dose at 6 weeks, 63.1% seroconverted. After the full primary series ending at 12 to 13 weeks, 92.2% had protective antibody and about 8% did not. A further MLV CPV dose at 15 to 16 weeks produced seroconversion in all 20 remaining non-responders tested. The authors recommend a final dose at 15 to 16 weeks.

Claims citing this study

  • Most puppies have responded by 12 to 16 weeks, and late non-responders respond to one more dose MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
