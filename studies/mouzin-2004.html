ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Mouzin 2004

Full reference
Mouzin DE, Lorenzen MJ, Haworth JD, King VL. 2004. Duration of serologic response to three viral antigens in cats. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 224(1):61-66.
DOI
10.2460/javma.2004.224.61
Design
serologic survey (pre- and post-revaccination titers, no challenge)
Species
cat
Animals
272 cats
Population
healthy client-owned cats 2 years or older, previously vaccinated, revaccinated with a vaccine from the line they had historically received (Felocell 4, Pfizer, per product footnote)
Funding
manufacturer. All authors at Pfizer Animal Health Biologicals R&D, and the vaccine line was Pfizer's (author affiliation, product footnote). No funding statement
Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome only, a 5 to 7 day post-vaccination sample, and manufacturer authorship.
What it reports
96.7% of cats either had an FPV HI titer of 1:40 or more or a 4-fold rise after revaccination, and the authors state the response lasted up to and beyond 48 months in most cats.
