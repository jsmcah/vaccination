ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Edinboro 1999

Full reference
Edinboro CH, Janowitz LK, Guptill-Yoran L, Glickman LT. 1999. A clinical trial of intranasal and subcutaneous vaccines to prevent upper respiratory infection in cats at an animal shelter. Feline Practice 27(6):7-13.
Design
experimental study (nonrandomized alternate-allocation shelter field trial)
Species
cat
Animals
90 cats enrolled, 57 with outcome data
Population
apparently healthy cats admitted to one California humane society, 1996
Funding
mixed. Kenneth Scott Fellowship supported the first author. Pfizer donated the IN vaccine (acknowledgments)
Study's main limitation
Not randomized or blinded, 33 of 90 lost to follow-up, pathogens not identified, and the SQ vaccine was killed.
What it reports
URI incidence was 54.8% with killed SQ FVRCP alone and 30.8% with SQ plus IN MLV FHV-FCV vaccine. Adjusted risk reduction was 76%. No vaccination history and purebred status raised URI risk.
