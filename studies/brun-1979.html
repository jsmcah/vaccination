ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Brun 1979

Full reference
Brun A, Chappuis G, Precausta P, Terre J. 1979. Immunisation against panleucopenia: early development of immunity. Comparative Immunology, Microbiology and Infectious Diseases 1(4):335-339.
DOI
10.1016/0147-9571(79)90034-1
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
cat
Animals
28 cats (16 vaccinated in groups of 2 or 4, plus 2 unvaccinated controls per test)
Population
cats from the company's protected breeding colony, 3 to 5 months old, born to seronegative dams and seronegative before vaccination
Funding
manufacturer. All authors at IFFA-Merieux, Lyon, maker of the vaccine tested (Feliniffa) (author affiliation). No funding statement
Study's main limitation
Two cats per time point for the intraperitoneal challenges, and the product was the authors' own company's vaccine.
What it reports
A single SQ MLV dose protected 2/2 cats against intraperitoneal challenge at 9, 6 and 3 days, but 0/2 at 2 days and 1/4 at 1 day. 4/4 cats vaccinated on day 0 and placed immediately with shedding diseased cats were protected.
