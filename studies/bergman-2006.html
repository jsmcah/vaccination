ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Bergman 2006

Full reference
Bergman JGHE, Muniz M, Sutton D, Fensome R, Ling F, Paul G. 2006. Comparative trial of the canine parvovirus, canine distemper virus and canine adenovirus type 2 fractions of two commercially available modified live vaccines. Veterinary Record 159(22):733-736.
DOI
10.1136/vr.159.22.733
Design
controlled serology study (two vaccine arms, no challenge)
Species
dog
Animals
39 beagle puppies (19 Nobivac DHPPi, 20 Duramune DHPPi+LC)
Population
commercial beagle puppies of regularly vaccinated bitches, vaccinated at 6 and 10 weeks
Funding
manufacturer. All authors were employed by Intervet, maker of one of the two vaccines compared (author affiliations)
Study's main limitation
Manufacturer comparison of its own product with a competitor, antibody outcome only, and a single beagle source.
What it reports
Median CPV maternal antibody titer at 4 weeks was 320. After vaccination at 6 weeks, 19 of 19 Nobivac puppies and 17 of 20 Duramune puppies responded to CPV, and the 3 non-responders responded after the 10 week dose. No serious adverse reactions were seen.

Claims citing this study

  • Many puppies respond to MLV CPV vaccine at 4 to 6 weeks despite maternal antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Maternal antibody can block MLV CAV-2, so puppies need repeat doses MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Maternal antibody can block MLV CAV-2, so puppies need repeat doses Intranasal MLV BAP or BP
