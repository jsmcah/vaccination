What it reports
States that VS-FCV kills up to two-thirds of affected cats (mortality up to 67%) and that vaccination did not protect against field infections in studies, though some experimental protection was achieved. Says vaccination gives good protection against acute oral and respiratory disease but does not prevent infection or shedding. Most recovered cats clear FCV in about 30 days, while a few shed much longer, possibly for life. FCV persists in the environment for about 1 month.
