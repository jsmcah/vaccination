ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Hess 2011

Full reference
Hess TJ, Parker DS, Hassall AJ, Chiang YW. 2011. Evaluation of efficacy of oral administration of Bordetella bronchiseptica intranasal vaccine when used to protect puppies from tracheobronchitis due to B bronchiseptica infection. International Journal of Applied Research in Veterinary Medicine 9(3):300-305.
Design
experimental challenge study (randomized complete block by litter, placebo controlled)
Species
dog
Animals
50 Beagle puppies enrolled (17 placebo, 17 multivalent, 16 monovalent), 44 challenged and analyzed (15, 15, 14)
Population
Bb sero and culture negative Beagle puppies vaccinated at 8 weeks, aerosol challenge with two pooled virulent Bb strains 5 weeks later
Funding
manufacturer. Boehringer Ingelheim Vetmedica funded the study, all authors are BI employees, and the multivalent vaccine (Bronchi-Shield III) is a BI product (acknowledgement and affiliations)
Study's main limitation
Manufacturer study supporting a label claim, with groups housed separately during the vaccination phase. Single challenge at 5 weeks in naive puppies, and observer blinding is not stated.
What it reports
A single oral (buccal pouch) dose of intranasal-labeled live avirulent Bb vaccines was tested. Tracheobronchitis occurred in 15/15 placebo, 0/15 multivalent (Bb/CPIV/CAV-2, Bronchi-Shield III) and 1/14 monovalent (Bronchi-Shield) puppies. Vaccine efficacy 1.0 (95% CI 0.78 to 1.00) and 0.92 (95% CI 0.66 to 1.00). All vaccinated puppies seroconverted and had tracheal Bb colonization after vaccination.
