Study's main limitation
Single-author narrative review without a stated search strategy, written by an investigator who has run industry-funded Bb vaccine studies, including Ellis 2001 which it cites as the main evidence for prime-boost.
What it reports
Concludes that IN, parenteral and oral Bb vaccines reduce Bb disease under laboratory conditions in seronegative puppies at peak immunity, but that many studies are flawed, chiefly by unaccounted pen effects, and that duration of immunity remains unresolved. Reinterprets Bey 1981 to place the earliest meaningful nasal IgA rise at day 6, not day 4, and notes no protection at 24 hours and none beyond controls at 48 hours. Suggests combined mucosal and parenteral vaccination may give the broadest protection.
