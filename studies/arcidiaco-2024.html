ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Arcidiaco 2024

Full reference
Arcidiaco S, Schreiber P, Poincelot L, Loukeri S, Lesbros C, Gueguen S. 2024. Vaccines efficacy against infection with circulating feline calicivirus after one single injection: Comparison of Leucofeligen FeLV/RCP and Purevax RCPFeLV vaccines. Vaccine 42(26):126393.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2024.126393
Design
experimental challenge study (two vaccines vs unvaccinated control, one dose, heterologous field-strain FCV challenge 3 weeks later, not blinded)
Species
cat
Animals
30 SPF kittens (10 Leucofeligen FeLV/RCP, 10 Purevax RCPFeLV, 10 unvaccinated controls)
Population
SPF European kittens 9 to 11 weeks old, randomized
Funding
manufacturer. All authors were employed by Virbac, which makes Leucofeligen, and each reports financial support from Virbac SA (acknowledgements and funding, competing interest declaration)
Study's main limitation
Head-to-head comparison run by the maker of the winning product, not blinded, 10 cats per group, and a single challenge strain.
What it reports
One SQ dose of Leucofeligen (MLV FCV F9 with adjuvanted FeLV fraction) lowered the mean maximal clinical score vs controls (P = 0.0021) and lowered nasal FCV titers vs controls on days 10, 11, 13 and 14 after challenge. Purevax (inactivated FCV 431 and G1) did not differ from controls on clinical score or shedding. All cats in all groups shed FCV from day 3. By day 21, 80% of Leucofeligen kittens had seroconverted and no Purevax kitten had.
