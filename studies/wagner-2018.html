ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Wagner 2018

Full reference
Wagner DC, Kass PH, Hurley KF. 2018. Cage size, movement in and out of housing during daily care, and other environmental and population health risk factors for feline upper respiratory disease in nine North American animal shelters. PLOS ONE 13(1):e0190140.
DOI
10.1371/journal.pone.0190140
Design
prospective observational multi-shelter study
Species
cat
Animals
nine shelters (329 cats sampled at intake in five)
Population
cats in nine North American shelters, all vaccinating with MLV parenteral FVRCP at intake
Funding
independent. Morris Animal Foundation D08FE-057 and Koret Shelter Medicine Program (funding statement). No competing interests
Study's main limitation
Shelter-level comparisons, so IN vaccine use may mark shelters with more URI.
What it reports
Housing moves and cage size were linked to URI. Shelter use of IN FVRC vaccine was associated with higher URI risk (IRR 1.66, 95% CI 1.13 to 2.44).
