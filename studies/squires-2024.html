Study's main limitation

What it reports
States that inactivated FPV vaccines typically do not provide protection as long-lasting as MLV vaccines. In shelters, it recommends MLV core vaccination at intake including pregnant animals, judging that rapid protection outweighs fetal risk. It notes no MLV core vaccine is approved for pregnant queens.
