ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Lechner 2010

Full reference
Lechner ES, Crawford PC, Levy JK, Edinboro CH, Dubovi EJ, Caligiuri R. 2010. Prevalence of protective antibody titers for canine distemper virus and canine parvovirus in dogs entering a Florida animal shelter. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 236(12):1317-1321.
DOI
10.2460/javma.236.12.1317
Design
cross-sectional serosurvey
Species
dog
Animals
431 dogs
Population
dogs admitted to an open-admission municipal shelter in north central Florida
Funding
independent. Maddie's Fund grant (footnote)
Study's main limitation
One shelter with a history of outbreaks. Puppies with maternal antibody are included.
What it reports
186 of 431 (43%) had protective CDV titers by virus neutralization at admission. Older and neutered dogs were more likely to have protective titers.

Claims citing this study

  • Roughly half of dogs entering shelters have no measurable CDV antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Many dogs arrive at shelters without protective CPV antibody MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • About two thirds of dogs arrive already protected, so population immunity starts high MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
