ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Moore 2005

Full reference
Moore GE, Guptill LF, Ward MP, Glickman NW, Faunt KK, Lewis HB, Glickman LT. 2005. Adverse events diagnosed within three days of vaccine administration in dogs. Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association 227(7):1102-1108.
DOI
10.2460/javma.2005.227.1102
Design
retrospective observational (cohort, practice records)
Species
dog
Animals
1,226,159 dogs
Population
dogs vaccinated at 360 Banfield hospitals, 2002 to 2003
Funding
independent. CDC National Center for Infectious Diseases grant
Study's main limitation
Events within 3 days only, from practice records.
What it reports
38.2 adverse events per 10,000 dogs overall. Rabies vaccine alone had 24.7 per 10,000 doses. Rates fell with body weight and rose with the number of vaccines given.
