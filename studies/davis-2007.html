ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Davis 2007

Full reference
Davis R, Jayappa H, Abdelmagid OY, Armstrong R, Sweeney D, Lehr C. 2007. Comparison of the mucosal immune response in dogs vaccinated with either an intranasal avirulent live culture or a subcutaneous antigen extract vaccine of Bordetella bronchiseptica. Veterinary Therapeutics 8(1):32-40.
Design
experimental challenge study (randomized, placebo controlled, observers masked)
Species
dog
Animals
35 puppies in three groups (IN trivalent, SC antigen extract given twice 21 days apart, saline placebo)
Population
healthy beagle puppies 9 to 10 weeks old with no Bb vaccination history and low Bb agglutinating antibody
Funding
manufacturer. Funded by Schering-Plough Animal Health, and all authors are Schering-Plough employees (funding footnote and affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Bordetella only, with no CPIV outcome. Single challenge 63 days after vaccination in a sponsor-run study with small groups.
What it reports
IN vaccinated dogs developed Bb-specific nasal IgA, while two SC doses of antigen extract induced no nasal IgA. After challenge on day 63, IN dogs coughed less than SC or placebo dogs, and SC dogs did not differ from placebo.
