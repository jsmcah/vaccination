Rodriguez 2023

Full reference

Design
case series

Funding

Study's main limitation
No unvaccinated comparison, many concurrent outbreak measures, and an inconsistent account of who was vaccinated.
What it reports
5 of 16 cats (31.3%) died or were euthanized, all 2 to 4 months old. The isolate was resistant to doxycycline and other drugs. Exposed cats were vaccinated with intranasal feline Bordetella vaccine 5 days after the first cases, along with clean break, quarantine and targeted antibiotics. One vaccinated affected cat had mild signs and four vaccinated high-risk cats stayed well. The outbreak resolved in 26 days.
