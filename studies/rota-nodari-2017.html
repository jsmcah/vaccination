Study's main limitation
Associations only. Failure is a titer below 0.5 IU/mL, which does not equal lack of protection.
What it reports
Failure was 13.15% in imported dogs and 5.89% in dogs vaccinated in Italy, and varied by vaccine, breed and sampling time.

Claims citing this study
