Study's main limitation
Expert opinion chapter in which several shelter recommendations (no revaccination of IN products, natural boosting in shelters) are stated without cited data.
What it reports
Classes IN or oral MLV Bb vaccine as core for dogs entering shelters, given on intake from as early as 4 weeks of age as a single dose for adults and puppies (Table 9.1). States that mucosal vaccines are not blocked by MDA, that innate protection begins within hours and specific local immunity in 3 to 7 days, and that oral Bb was as efficacious as IN in the authors' study (Larson 2013). Says revaccination of shelter puppies is unlikely to help because most will be naturally exposed within 2 to 4 weeks, and that the killed parenteral product needs two doses 2 to 4 weeks apart and should not be given IN.
