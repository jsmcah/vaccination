ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Schultz 1973

Full reference
Schultz RD, Scott FW. 1973. Absence of an immune response after oral administration of attenuated feline panleukopenia virus. Infection and Immunity 7(4):547-549.
DOI
10.1128/iai.7.4.547-549.1973
Design
experimental vaccination and challenge study (route comparison)
Species
cat
Animals
27 cats (15 oral, 7 IN or oral-IN, 5 SQ), adults, adolescents and newborn kittens
Population
cats and newborn kittens at Cornell
Funding
government. Public Health Service grant AI-10042, NIAID (acknowledgments)
Study's main limitation
Very small route groups mixing newborn and adolescent cats, and the main aim was the oral route.
What it reports
Oral MLV FPV produced no systemic or local antibody, and orally vaccinated seronegative cats became ill on challenge. IN and SQ vaccination produced high neutralizing antibody and, per the abstract, protection from virulent challenge.

Claims citing this study

  • SQ and IN vaccines produced similar antibody responses and similar protection against challenge MLV FVRCP
