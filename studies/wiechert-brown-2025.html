Wiechert-Brown 2025

Full reference
Wiechert-Brown SA, Classe HM, Dant JC, LaFleur RL, Xu Z, Tarpey I. 2025. One year duration of immunity of a combination Bordetella bronchiseptica - canine parainfluenza oral vaccine in dogs. Frontiers in Veterinary Science 12:1634190.
DOI
10.3389/fvets.2025.1634190
Design
experimental challenge study (randomized, placebo-controlled)
Species
dog
Animals
46 dogs in the CPIV study (23 vaccinated, 23 placebo), plus a separate Bordetella study
Population
purpose-bred beagles 6 to 8 weeks old at oral vaccination
Funding
manufacturer. All authors are Merck Animal Health or MSD Animal Health employees, maker of the product. Paper declares no funding and no conflicts
Study's main limitation
Single manufacturer study in which CPIV challenge caused no disease in controls, so the CPIV outcome is shedding.
What it reports
One year after one oral dose, CPIV shedding was lower in vaccinates (0.2 versus 1.1 log10 FAID50 per mL). Bordetella challenge caused disease in 9% of vaccinates versus 74% of controls.
