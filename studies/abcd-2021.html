Study's main limitation
Consensus review. Its summaries of studies not in Zotero (Jirjis 2010, Wilson 2012, Patel 2015) are secondary.
What it reports
Calls FeLV vaccine non-core but recommended for cats at risk. Says no FeLV vaccine gives complete protection or prevents regressive infection, gives two doses 3 to 4 weeks apart then a booster at 1 year, and boosters every 2 to 3 years in cats over 3 to 4 years. Advises testing before vaccination and testing before group housing in shelters.
