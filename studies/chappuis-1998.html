Study's main limitation

What it reports
Five SPF beagle pups given MLV CPV (Cornell strain) at 1 day old had no reported adverse reaction and developed antibody comparable to older pups. Vaccination at birth before colostrum immunized 3 pups, but vaccination after colostrum or in pups of high-titer dams failed. States maternal antibody falls to 1% to 3% of its initial value by about 30 days in dogs and cats, and CPV maternal antibody can be detected up to 15 weeks.
