Full reference
Balboni A, Mollace C, Giunti M, Dondi F, Prosperi S, Battilani M. 2014. Investigation of the presence of canine adenovirus (CAdV) in owned dogs in Northern Italy. Research in Veterinary Science 97(3):631-636.

Study's main limitation
Small convenience sample at one hospital over 5 weeks, with PCR positivity rather than disease as the outcome.
What it reports
CAV-2 DNA in 30 of 51 dogs (58.8%) and CAV-1 in 4 (7.8%), all four also CAV-2 positive. 44 of 51 were vaccinated. CAV-1 sequences matched Italian fox and dog strains, which the authors read as a genetically stable strain shared with wild canids.
