Study's main limitation
Antibody outcome only, in owned and mostly previously vaccinated dogs, with no challenge.
What it reports
86.0% had HI titers of 1:80 or more before vaccination. After one dose of Nobivac SHP (MSD), 83.0% showed no titer increase and 17.0% had a fourfold rise, 88.2% of them by day 7. Of 14 dogs without protective titers, 7 rose fourfold, and one dog never developed detectable antibody. Transient side effects were reported in 37.0% of dogs, mostly lethargy (17%) and gastrointestinal signs (12%).

Claims citing this study

  • Most adult dogs already have high CPV titers, and few show a rise after a booster MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Reported CPV vaccine side effects are transient lethargy, fever and injection-site soreness MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
