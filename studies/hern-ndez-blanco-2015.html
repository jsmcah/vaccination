ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Hernández-Blanco 2015

Full reference
Hernández-Blanco B, Catala-López F. 2015. Are licensed canine parvovirus (CPV2 and CPV2b) vaccines able to elicit protection against CPV2c subtype in puppies? A systematic review of controlled clinical trials. Veterinary Microbiology 180(1-2):1-9.
DOI
10.1016/j.vetmic.2015.07.027
Design
narrative review
Species
dog
Animals
6 trials with 109 puppies in total (7 to 30 per trial)
Population
controlled challenge trials of licensed CPV-2 or CPV-2b vaccines against CPV-2c in puppies
Funding
independent. Review states no funding. The included trials were industry funded or industry authored where reported (table 1)
Study's main limitation
Systematic review of six small, mostly industry trials with poorly reported randomization and blinding.
What it reports
Most included trials reported benefits in clinical signs, serology and shedding, but maternal antibody was not considered in 5 of 6. The authors concluded no final conclusion on cross-protection against CPV-2c was possible and called for specifically designed trials.
