Study's main limitation

What it reports
Calls Bordetella a primary feline pathogen and non-core. Says dogs with respiratory disease are a risk for cats. Encourages vaccination in shelters, particularly with a history of confirmed disease. States live vaccine should never be given under 4 weeks, is ineffective in cats on antibiotics, may cause mild signs, and gives at least one year of immunity.
