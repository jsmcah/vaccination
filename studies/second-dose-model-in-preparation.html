Study's main limitation
Direct protection only, no transmission effects; constant daily disease risk; the share of first-dose failures a second dose rescues is bounded, not measured; results to be posted with the preprint
What it reports
Counts second doses given per additional case averted. An adult benefits only if it was unprotected at intake, its first dose failed, the second dose works, and it is still in the shelter and well when that dose takes effect. Its benefit depends mainly on the shelter's rate of adult cases diagnosed 8 to 21 days after intake, and on how often a first dose fails and a second dose succeeds. Once that case rate is known, length of stay changes doses per case averted little, though longer stays avert more cases per admission because more animals are present, and where adult disease is common most of the additional cases occur in animals a second dose cannot protect. A shelter can apply it using that case rate and its mean and median adult stay.

Claims citing this study

  • A second dose may protect a small number of additional adults in shelters where diagnosed adult cases arising in the shelter occur regularly MLV FVRCP
  • A second dose may protect a few additional adults where adult CDV cases arising in the shelter are elevated MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • A second dose may protect a few more adults in shelters where adult CPV cases arising in the shelter occur regularly MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
