ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Wilson 2013

Full reference
Wilson S, Stirling C, Thomas A, King V, Plevová E, Chromá L, Siedek E, Illambas J, Salt J, Sture G. 2013. Duration of immunity of a multivalent (DHPPi/L4R) canine vaccine against four Leptospira serovars. Vaccine 31(31):3126-3130.
DOI
10.1016/j.vaccine.2013.05.043
Design
experimental challenge study (four studies, vaccinate vs control)
Species
dog
Animals
24 vaccinated dogs plus unvaccinated controls across four studies
Population
puppies vaccinated twice from 6 weeks of age, 3 weeks apart, challenged about 1 year later
Funding
manufacturer. Authors at Zoetis and Bioveta (author affiliations)
Study's main limitation
Two doses only, so it does not test a single dose or onset. European serovars (Bratislava in place of Pomona). Small groups
What it reports
No vaccinated dog showed clinical signs after challenge with Canicola, Icterohaemorrhagiae, Bratislava or Grippotyphosa at about 1 year. 5 of 24 vaccinated dogs had positive blood cultures and 1 shed in urine, versus all controls. Antibody appeared 21 days after the first dose and peaked 3 to 6 weeks after the second

Claims citing this study

  • Two doses 3 to 4 weeks apart are needed, and protection begins 1 to 2 weeks after the second Leptospirosis (quadrivalent)
  • Protection lasts at least 12 to 15 months after the two-dose series, so revaccination is annual Leptospirosis (quadrivalent)
