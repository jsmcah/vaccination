ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Gerber 1976

Full reference
Gerber JD, Marron AE. 1976. Cell-mediated immunity and age at vaccination associated with measles inoculation and protection of dogs against canine distemper. American Journal of Veterinary Research 37(2):133-138.
Design
experimental study
Species
dog
Animals
33 Beagles
Population
colony Beagles, 18 months old, 50 to 64 days old, or 16 to 30 days old
Funding
manufacturer. Authors at Norden Laboratories (affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Four to five dogs per age group, no challenge, and harms were not assessed.
What it reports
Dogs given CDV vaccine made cell-mediated and antibody responses to CDV, while measles-vaccinated dogs made only a cell-mediated response to CDV. Puppies vaccinated at 16 to 30 days, which had maternal antibody, made neither response. Littermates vaccinated at 50 to 64 days responded.

Claims citing this study

  • Measles vaccine induced only cell-mediated, not antibody, responses to CDV MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • Puppies given CDV vaccine at 16 to 30 days old made no detectable immune response MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
