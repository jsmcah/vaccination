ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Larson 2008

Full reference
Larson LJ, Schultz RD. 2008. Do two current canine parvovirus type 2 and 2b vaccines provide protection against the new type 2c variant? Veterinary Therapeutics 9(2).
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
28 pups (9 Galaxy DA2PPv CPV-2b, 9 Continuum DAP CPV-2, 10 saline controls)
Population
12-week-old CPV antibody-negative beagle pups
Funding
manufacturer. Funded by Intervet/Schering-Plough Animal Health, maker of both vaccines tested (funding statement)
Study's main limitation
Single challenge 5 weeks after one dose with a mixed 2b and 2c inoculum, small groups, and manufacturer funding.
What it reports
After one dose, all vaccinated pups had HI antibody and none showed disease after combined CPV-2b and CPV-2c challenge 5 weeks later, while all controls became ill and 50% died or were euthanized. Virus was found in the feces of 2 of 9 CPV-2 vaccinates, attributed by the authors to coprophagy.

Claims citing this study

  • Current CPV-2 and CPV-2b vaccines protect against the CPV-2a, 2b and 2c field variants MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
  • One MLV dose protected all seronegative dogs against challenge 3 to 5 weeks later MLV DAPP, DAP, or DP
