ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

André-Fontaine 2003

Full reference
André-Fontaine G, Branger C, Gray AW, Klaasen HLBM. 2003. Comparison of the efficacy of three commercial bacterins in preventing canine leptospirosis. Veterinary Record 153(6):165-169.
DOI
10.1136/vr.153.6.165
Design
randomized challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
24 (6 per group; group B lost 2, leaving 4)
Population
SPF Beagles vaccinated at 9 and 12 weeks with Vanguard 7, Dohyvac 7L or Nobivac DHPPi + Lepto, or a non-lepto control
Funding
manufacturer. Two authors worked for Intervet, which makes vaccine C, and vaccination took place in Intervet's facilities (affiliations, methods)
Study's main limitation
Six dogs per group, one serogroup (Canicola) challenge 7 weeks after the series, and two authors from the maker of the best-performing vaccine.
What it reports
After Canicola challenge, blood and urine cultures were positive in all controls. Blood cultures were positive in 1 of 6 (vaccine A), 2 of 4 (vaccine B) and 0 of 6 (vaccine C), and urine cultures in the same numbers. Clinical signs were mild in vaccinates. Only a mild MAT response followed the second dose.

Claims citing this study

  • Commercial vaccines gave about 84% protection against clinical disease and 88% against renal carriage in trials Leptospirosis (quadrivalent)
