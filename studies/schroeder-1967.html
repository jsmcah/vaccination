ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Schroeder 1967

Full reference
Schroeder JP, Bordt DW, Mitchell FE. 1967. Studies of canine distemper immunization of puppies in a canine distemper-contaminated environment. Veterinary Medicine, Small Animal Clinician 62(8):782-787.
Design
experimental challenge study (natural exposure)
Species
dog
Animals
21 vaccinated and 16 unvaccinated puppies in the main experiment
Population
farm-raised puppies 10 to 14 weeks old placed in a kennel of about 400 dogs with distemper
Funding
manufacturer. Authors at Pitman-Moore, Dow Chemical, maker of the vaccine (affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Three small experiments with one dog per condition in places, and the company's own vaccine.
What it reports
One of 21 vaccinated puppies and 14 of 16 unvaccinated littermates died of distemper. Puppies with maternal antibody fared worse. The authors conclude that delayed vaccination greatly reduces the chance of resisting infection.
