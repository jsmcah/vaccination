ASV shelter vaccination guidelines: evidence

Emery 1976

Full reference
Emery JB, House JA, Bittle JL, Spotts AM. 1976. A canine parainfluenza viral vaccine. Immunogenicity and safety. American Journal of Veterinary Research 37(11):1323-1327.
Design
experimental challenge study
Species
dog
Animals
20 vaccinated and 5 control dogs in the challenge test, plus smaller immunogenicity groups
Population
seronegative dogs given 2 SQ doses 4 weeks apart
Funding
manufacturer. Authors at the Biological Research Division of Pitman-Moore Inc (affiliation)
Study's main limitation
Small control group, two doses only, and a challenge that produced only mild disease.
What it reports
Six days after challenge 2 weeks after the second dose, 3 of 20 vaccinates and 5 of 5 controls shed virus, and none of the vaccinates and 80% of controls shed on day 7. Mild disease in 3 of 5 controls and no vaccinate. IM gave higher antibody than SQ.
