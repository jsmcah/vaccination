Note: H3N8 influenza has been eradicated in dogs

Considerations

  • H3N2 influenza is occasionally responsible for temporary community and shelter outbreaks in North America, but is not circulating or endemic. The use of this vaccine in shelter outbreaks requires consideration of several factors, discussed here. H3N2 vaccination of at-risk, social community dogsmay be considered when H3N2 influenza has been locally identified, clinic resources allow *and *follow-up revaccination appointments are guaranteed.

What the labels mean

Which vaccines

Core
Considered essential for all animals in that context category, and should be given at intake or as soon as possible. (Guideline definition)
Situational
May have good evidence for use under particular circumstances, but not appropriate for all shelter or community animals. (Guideline definition)

How certain the evidence is

Evidence rating
The task force's own rating of the evidence behind each recommendation, from 0 to 4, recorded when the recommendations were voted. Higher means more certain. A range means the statement rests on more than one voted recommendation and they were rated differently.
Not rated
No rating was recorded for this statement, usually because it was proposed after the vote.

Kinds of evidence

  • ■ Experimental
  • ● Observational
  • ▲ Case report or series
  • ◆ Expert or consensus
  • ■ filled: independent or not stated   □ hollow: manufacturer funded, authored, or supplied
  • (species) study in a different species
  • (model) decision model
  • Italic no study cited: Inference (reasoned from cited claims), Consensus (the task force's judgment), Not assembled (an evidence gap)

Response to a single dose

ClaimCitation
Few dogs have antibody 14 days after one dose of inactivated H3N2 vaccine □ (dog)
Limits of the evidence for this claim
On day 14, 1 of 10 (higher antigen dose) and 0 of 10 (standard dose) had HI antibody. By day 21, 7 of 10 and 2 of 10 had. Antibody, not protection
One dose given 7 days before contact exposure lowered clinical scores over 2 to 4 weeks but not over the first week □ (dog)
Limits of the evidence for this claim
Abstract only, 8 vaccinated and 8 control beagles, clinical scores only, with no serology or virology. A Zoetis author and Zoetis vaccine

Harms

ClaimCitation
CIV vaccines have adverse event rates no higher than other canine vaccines ● (dog), □ (dog), □ (dog)
Limits of the evidence for this claim
Moore 2023 gives 15.1 per 10,000 doses (95% CI 13.8 to 16.4), but the adjusted OR versus visits without CIV vaccine was 1.01 (95% CI 0.92 to 1.12). Field safety trials were uncontrolled and manufacturer run

Draws on the biology of: Onset of protection, Efficacy, Response to a single dose

Balance. Exposed dogs are already infected by diagnosis, and vaccinating them may hide subclinical shedders. Unexposed dogs kept fully apart may gain partial protection if both doses come first. Favors vaccinating only a true clean break.
