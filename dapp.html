Considerations

  • Modified live DAPP, DAP, or DP vaccination is strongly recommended at or before intake for all shelter dogs, and at the first encounter for those attending community clinics. Shelter-housed puppies should receive a first vaccine by 4 weeks of age, but may be vaccinated earlier where risk of canine distemper or parvovirus is meaningfully elevated. For puppies living in shelters or high-risk environments, MLV DAPP should be repeated every 2 weeks until shelter exit or 20 weeks old. For puppies in low-risk foster or community homes, DAPP can be started at 4-6 weeks, and can be repeated every 2-4 weeks until shelter exit or 20 weeks old. For adult dogs, except where lengths of stay are >30 days and the risk of parvovirus or canine distemper is meaningfully elevated, one vaccine dose is sufficient.
  • Note: DP should only be used when the intranasal vaccine that includes adenovirus is also given (see below).

What the labels mean

Which vaccines

Core
Considered essential for all animals in that context category, and should be given at intake or as soon as possible. (Guideline definition)
Situational
May have good evidence for use under particular circumstances, but not appropriate for all shelter or community animals. (Guideline definition)

How certain the evidence is

Evidence rating
The task force's own rating of the evidence behind each recommendation, from 0 to 4, recorded when the recommendations were voted. Higher means more certain. A range means the statement rests on more than one voted recommendation and they were rated differently.
Not rated
No rating was recorded for this statement, usually because it was proposed after the vote.

Kinds of evidence

  • ■ Experimental
  • ● Observational
  • ▲ Case report or series
  • ◆ Expert or consensus
  • ■ filled: independent or not stated   □ hollow: manufacturer funded, authored, or supplied
  • (species) study in a different species
  • (model) decision model
  • Italic no study cited: Inference (reasoned from cited claims), Consensus (the task force's judgment), Not assembled (an evidence gap)
