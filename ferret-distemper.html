Considerations

  • Canine distemper virus (recombinant, MLV)vaccination in ferrets is complicated by the use of cultured ferret cells to attenuate vaccines targeted for dogs. Vaccination of a ferret with a canine DAPP product could result in death[22]. Ferrets should be vaccinated with a ferret-safe canine distemper vaccine (see list above) at or before shelter entry, or at the first available encounter in community clinics. If no ferret-safe vaccine is available, adoption or transfer with a disclaimer that the animal is unvaccinated is recommended.
  • Vaccine reactions are significantly more common in ferrets than dogs and cats; those administering vaccines should have a plan for managing reactions, including both preemptive and reactive protocols.
